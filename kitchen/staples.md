## What the two labels already say

**Breakfast is a protein hole.** Three packets of the maple oatmeal come to **480 kcal, 12 g protein, 99 g carbs, 36 g added sugar**. That's 16% of the ~3,000 kcal day but only ~9% of the ~140 g protein target. A 185 lb hiker needs more than that before a dawn start. Fixes, in order of least hassle, once their labels are in:
1. Stir in whole milk powder. (The old Kentucky oat bags already had 25 g; did that happen this trip?)
2. Peanut butter or roasted nuts (roasted is confirmed fine for the OAS).
3. Switch to plain instant oats, which lose the 12 g of added sugar per packet, and add the above.

**Brami is the right dinner base.** 4 oz dry (two label servings) is **400 kcal, 24 g protein, 72 g carbs** before sauce and meat. That's about twice the protein of ordinary pasta, and it's why the nightly Brami dinner works. With a chicken pouch it probably lands around 50+ g protein, but the pouch label decides that.
