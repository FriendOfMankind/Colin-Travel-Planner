The pitch · summer 2027

Oregon, August 1–12

Colin is already out west with the car after Glacier. Twelve days, one loop from Portland: tide pools and sand dunes on the coast, hot springs on the McKenzie River, a volcano and a river float around Bend, then two rafting days and a waterfall tunnel in the Columbia River Gorge. Come for all of it, or pick the days you want.

Oregon in one minute

The coast
Oregon's whole coastline is public. Black basalt headlands stick out into the Pacific, and on a few summer mornings the tide drops low enough to walk out over rock that is underwater the rest of the year.
Two Oregons
The Cascade Mountains split the state in two. West of them it's wet and green; an hour east it turns to dry high desert, sagebrush and rimrock canyons. This trip crosses that line twice.
The volcanoes
The Cascades are a chain of volcanoes. Mt. Hood watches over the Gorge, and the Three Sisters stand west of Bend. South Sister is the tallest of the three, and the one we climb.
Lava country
Around McKenzie Pass, lava flows covered the land in black rock. One flow dammed a river and drowned a forest about 3,000 years ago. That's Clear Lake, and the dead trees are still standing underwater.
The Gorge
The Columbia River cuts straight through the Cascades here, leaving an 80-mile canyon of basalt cliffs. The Ice Age floods that carved it left waterfalls pouring off nearly every side canyon.
Hot springs
Volcanic heat means natural hot springs. We sleep at one and soak at another. Belknap requires swimsuits; Terwilliger is clothing-optional.

Day by day

Every hike, jump and drop is optional. Drive times aren't routed yet unless a time is given.

Sun Aug 1 · land at PDX

Straight to the ocean

  • Airport pickup, then west to Newport on the central coast (about 2 h 20 m).
Sleep: Newport (motel not picked yet)
Mon Aug 2 · dawn start

Tide pools and spouting rock

  • Yaquina Head tide pools at the lowest tide of the summer: −2.3 ft at 6:59 AM, after a 6:04 sunrise. Rock that's underwater almost all year comes out, with sea stars, anemones and urchins in the pools.
  • Depoe Bay, where about 200 gray whales feed close to shore all summer.
  • Thor's Well on the incoming tide: a hole in the rock that the ocean pours into and sprays out of.
Sleep: Florence or Yachats (not picked yet)
Tue Aug 3

Dunes, then hot springs

  • The Oregon Dunes, the biggest coastal dunes in North America. Either drive a side-by-side on a guided 2-hour dune, forest and beach loop (18+ to drive), or ride in a sandrail.
  • Over the mountains to Belknap Hot Springs and soak until the pool closes.
Sleep: Belknap Hot Springs Lodge (night 1 of 2)
Wed Aug 4

Blue water

  • A rowboat on Clear Lake over a forest that drowned when lava dammed the lake about 3,000 years ago.
  • Tamolitch Blue Pool, where the river comes back up out of the lava.
  • Terwilliger Hot Springs at dusk: rock pools down a forested hillside. Clothing-optional, fair warning.
Sleep: Belknap Hot Springs Lodge (night 2 of 2)
Thu Aug 5

Lava, jumps and rock

  • Over McKenzie Pass through miles of black lava.
  • Steelhead Falls: a desert swimming hole with ledges to jump from.
  • Smith Rock at golden hour. Misery Ridge is a steep climb; the river trail below it is flat.
Sleep: Holiday Motel, Bend (night 1 of 3)
Fri Aug 6 · dawn start

South Sister

  • The summit of a 10,358-ft volcano: about 12.5 miles round trip and 5,000 ft of climbing, trail the whole way, no ropes. Long, steep, loose cinder near the top.
Easy option: float the river through Bend, or take it slow in town. Sleep: Holiday Motel, Bend (night 2 of 3)
Sat Aug 7 · off day

Float through Bend

  • Sleep in, then two hours on a tube through the middle of town. Also our spare day if smoke or weather moves something.
Sleep: Holiday Motel, Bend (night 3 of 3)
Sun Aug 8 · raft 1

Desert whitewater

  • Lower Deschutes, full day out of Maupin: 13 miles of Class III–IV through a desert canyon, lunch, swim stops and a 12-ft jump at Pirate's Cove. Then on to Hood River.
Sleep: Sunset Motel, Hood River (night 1 of 4)
Mon Aug 9 · raft 2

The waterfall drop

  • White Salmon River, a half-day of Class III–IV ending at Husum Falls, a 10–12 ft drop. Ride it or walk around and watch (16+).
  • Lost Lake at golden hour, with Mt. Hood over the water.
Sleep: Sunset Motel, Hood River (night 2 of 4)
Tue Aug 10

Behind Tunnel Falls

  • About 12 miles round trip on a trail cut into the canyon wall, with cable handholds, a bridge 150 ft up and a tunnel behind a 175-ft waterfall. If the canyon is closed in 2027, we swap in another waterfall hike.
Easy option: Punch Bowl Falls is 2 miles in, with a swim. Turn around there. Sleep: Sunset Motel, Hood River (night 3 of 4)
Wed Aug 11 · spare day

Nothing booked

  • Catch-up day for anything smoke or weather moved. Rowena Crest at sunset.
Sleep: Sunset Motel, Hood River (night 4 of 4)
Thu Aug 12

Fly home from Portland

  • About an hour to PDX from Hood River.

Good to know

The tides set the start

The best low tides of the whole summer fall on August 1–4, and by August 9 they're gone. That's why the coast comes first. Coming later still gets you everything else.

Smoke

Wildfire smoke hits Oregon in some Augusts. Rooms stay refundable where we can, and Aug 7 and Aug 11 are spare days.

The ocean

Cold, and not for swimming. Watch for sneaker waves and never turn your back on the water on the rocks. Summer highs on the coast are in the upper 60s; bring layers for the dawn tide pools.

Weather inland

Hot and dry around Bend, warm in the Gorge, cool nights. The South Sister summit is cold and windy even in August.

The water

Rivers and lakes are cold, the White Salmon especially. You need to be able to swim for the rafts and the jumps.

Phones and time

Signal is patchy outside towns, so download offline maps. Pacific time, 3 hours behind Cleveland.
