Dec 21–24: Finn and me, no car

The county bus (Summit Stage) is free and runs between Frisco, Breckenridge, Silverthorne, Keystone and Copper, roughly 6 AM to 1:30 AM. It stops at several trailheads. From the airport, the A Line train goes to Denver Union Station, then the Bustang bus to Frisco costs $12–13.

  • Mon 21Dec

    Fly in, bus up

    A morning flight (United 8:30 → 10:07 or Southwest 5:40 → 7:15), then the train and the bus. On Mondays the bus reaches Frisco at 12:40 or 2:40. An easy walk around town: day one at 9,100 ft is for adjusting.

  • Tue 22Dec

    My first lesson

    A private Nordic lesson at the Frisco Nordic Center, then the rest of the day on its 30 km of groomed trail. The lesson includes the day pass. Finn already skis.

    $95 lesson with pass · ~$35 ski rental

  • Wed 23Dec

    Ski to Breckenridge

    The rec path is groomed on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays: 8 miles from the Frisco Nordic Center to the Breckenridge Recreation Center. The free bus brings us back.

  • Thu 24Dec

    Mount Royal and Masontown

    Straight up from the end of Main Street on foot: about 1,250 ft in 2 miles, past the remains of an old mining camp, to the summit you can see from town. Or the Peaks Trail, 7.8 miles through the forest to Breckenridge, with the bus back.

Dec 25–30: skiing for everyone

Cross-country skiing is walking on skis on groomed track. Beginners are gliding within an hour. The Frisco Nordic Center is at the edge of town, with about 30 km of groomed ski trail and 8 km of snowshoe trail. A day pass there also works at Breckenridge Nordic and Gold Run.

  • Fri 25Dec

    You arrive

    Christmas. Take it easy: the first day at altitude is for water and naps.

  • Sat 26Dec

    Lesson day at Frisco Nordic

    A private lesson for the beginners, 1 hour 15 minutes, at 10:00, 12:00 or 2:00. All lessons there are private. The lesson includes a full-day trail pass, so we keep skiing afterward. Finn and I ski with you after.

    $95 per person lesson · ~$35 classic ski rental · book ahead by phone for more than one person: 970-668-2570

  • Sun 27Dec

    Snowshoe to Mayflower Gulch

    15–20 minutes up CO-91 by car. About 3–4 miles round trip on snowshoes, gaining 550–600 ft, to the old Boston Mine cabins under a big bowl. A midday outing: sunset is around 4:45.

  • Mon 28Dec

    Ski from Frisco to Breckenridge

    The same 8-mile groomed path, freshly groomed on Monday. Anyone tired rides the free bus for part of it. Lunch in Breckenridge.

    The path is free · rentals ~$35 each

  • Tue 29Dec

    Free day

    Another ski day at Frisco Nordic, Breckenridge Nordic or Gold Run, or Finn goes downhill. With the car, an option: Leadville's Mineral Belt Trail, an 11.6-mile groomed loop around the old mines, about 40 minutes away.

  • Wed 30Dec

    Home together

    Everyone drives to the airport and flies home, in time for New Year's Eve.

Rough ski cost for a beginner: the lesson day is about $130 ($95 lesson with pass + ~$35 rental), and the Breckenridge day is about $35 in rentals. Day passes are $35 for ages 13–64 and $30 for 65 and up.

What could go wrong, and the plan for it

  • Avalanches. Mount Royal is steep, and Mayflower Gulch sits under avalanche slopes. We check the Colorado Avalanche Information Center (avalanche.state.co.us) the morning of and stay on the trail. The Boston Mine cabins aren't a safe place to linger.
  • Altitude. Frisco is at 9,100 ft. Headaches and bad sleep are normal for a night or two. Water, and nothing hard on the first day.
  • Cold and short days. Single digits in the morning, sunset around 4:45. Start late morning and finish by mid-afternoon.
