Frisco week: skiing for everyone

Cross-country skiing is walking on skis on groomed track. Beginners are gliding within an hour. The Frisco Nordic Center is at the edge of town, at about 9,100 ft, with about 30 km of groomed ski trail and 8 km of snowshoe trail. It's projected to open Nov 26.

  • Fri 25Dec

    Arrive, take it easy

    Frisco is at 9,100 ft. Drink water and skip anything hard on day one.

  • Sat 26Dec

    Lesson day at Frisco Nordic

    A private lesson, 1 hour 15 minutes, at 10:00, 12:00 or 2:00. All lessons there are private. The lesson includes a full-day trail pass, so we keep skiing afterward. Finn already skis and can skip the lesson and just ski.

    $95 per person lesson · ~$35 classic ski rental · book ahead by phone for more than one person: 970-668-2570

  • Sun 27Dec

    Snowshoe to Mayflower Gulch

    15–20 minutes up CO-91 from Frisco. About 3–4 miles round trip on snowshoes, gaining 550–600 ft, to the old Boston Mine cabins under a big bowl. A midday outing: sunset is around 4:45.

    Snowshoe rental price at Frisco Nordic: not checked yet

  • Mon 28Dec

    Ski from Frisco to Breckenridge

    The town's recreation path is groomed for skiing on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays: 8 miles, point to point, from the Frisco Nordic Center to the Breckenridge Recreation Center. Monday means fresh grooming. One person drives around to pick us up, or anyone tired skis part of it and rides the rest. Lunch in Breckenridge.

    The path is free · rentals ~$35 each · a day pass is needed only where it crosses the Nordic Center trails

  • Tue 29Dec

    Free day

    Another ski day at Frisco Nordic, Breckenridge Nordic or Gold Run. A day pass at one works at all three. Or Finn goes downhill. A splurge option: a guided snowmobile tour to Georgia Pass on the Continental Divide, listed from $258 per person (not checked for these dates).

  • Wed 30Dec

    Everyone drives to Denver

    You two fly home. Finn and I keep going (the car plan is below).

Family ski cost, roughly: the lesson day is about $130 per beginner ($95 lesson with pass + ~$35 rental) and about $70 for Finn (pass + rental). The Breckenridge day is about $35 each in rentals. Day passes are $35 for ages 13–64 and $30 for 65 and up.

The car

Best: one car for the whole trip

If the Frisco rental isn't booked yet: one SUV at Denver airport (DEN), Dec 25 → Jan 4 (or 6), in my name, with you as extra drivers. On Dec 30 I drop you at departures and drive straight on. One rental, no second counter, and longer rentals usually cost less per day.

Hertz waives the under-25 fee for AAA members aged 20–24, and AAA members get a free additional driver. A 10-day price isn't checked yet.

Or: a second car on Dec 30

You return the Frisco car at DEN as planned. Finn and I pick up our own SUV at the same rental center and return it to DEN on Jan 4 (or 6). Same airport both ends, so no one-way fee.

Kayak, Nov 8: an SUV for Dec 30 → Jan 4 was $322–465 all in; Dec 30 → Jan 6 was $286–480. Without AAA, add Hertz's under-25 fee: typically $25–29 a day.

It needs to be an SUV with all-wheel drive. Colorado's traction law can apply on I-70 and on Berthoud Pass in snow, and "SUV" on a rental site doesn't guarantee AWD, so I'll confirm it at booking.

  • Mon 4Jan

    A shorter morning in the park, then about 1.5 hours to DEN.

  • Mon 4Jan

    Sky Pond, if it's safe

    About 10 miles past Mills Lake and The Loch. Timberline Falls is a frozen step in winter. We go only if the avalanche report is good that morning, and we turn around if it looks wrong.

  • Tue 5Jan

    Spare day

    Room for a storm or a closed road. If the weather's fine: Mills Lake and The Loch, or a third ski day.

  • Wed 6Jan

    Fly home

    About 1.5 hours to DEN.

What could go wrong, and the plan for it

  • Roads close in storms. I-70 over Vail Pass, the Eisenhower Tunnel approach and Berthoud Pass all close sometimes. We check COtrip before each drive. The 7-night version has a spare day for this.
  • Avalanches. The lake hikes run below avalanche slopes. We check the Colorado Avalanche Information Center (avalanche.state.co.us) every morning and stay on the trail.
  • Cold. Expect single digits in the morning. We have layers, and every day ends at a motel.
