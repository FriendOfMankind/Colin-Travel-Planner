The week, day by day

  1. Fri 23Pickup

    Bozeman to Many Glacier

    The long drive north across Montana, with a grocery stop in Great Falls (the last real store before the park). Arrive with hours of daylight left and walk a flat lap of Swiftcurrent Lake.

    ~7 h driving (estimate) · walk 2.6 mi · 131 ft
  2. Sat 24

    Easy Saturday

    Saturday is the park's busiest day, so everything starts at the campground. Redrock Falls, and Bullhead Lake further up the same valley if your legs want it.

    3.7 mi · 255 ft  /  or 6.8 mi · 446 ft
  3. Sun 25

    Iceberg Lake

    A lake in a 3,000-ft rock bowl that stays cold enough for icebergs to float in it into August. 5 AM start, for the parking and the light. Then the afternoon off.

    9.7 mi · 1,453 ft
  4. Mon 26

    Grinnell Glacier the big one

    The hardest day of the week, up to the glacier the park is famous for. A boat across two lakes cuts about 3.4 miles off if you want the shorter version. Hard turnaround at 10:30 AM wherever we are.

    11.1 mi · 2,047 ft (about 7.7 mi with the boat)
  5. Tue 27

    Going-to-the-Sun Road

    Over the pass to the west side, the drive people come to Glacier for. Then Avalanche Lake, with waterfalls pouring into it from three sides.

    ~2.5 h driving · 6.1 mi · 780 ft
  6. Wed 28

    The Highline the big one

    The first mile is a ledge cut into a cliff wall, with a hand cable and a long drop. Then miles along the wall to a backcountry chalet that sells snacks, and a long descent. One way, using the park shuttle. Not feeling the ledge? Hidden Lake Overlook instead (2.8 mi), and that's a good day too.

    11.8 mi · +2,388 ft · −4,773 ft
  7. Thu 29

    Slack day

    Held open for whatever got rained out or sold out. If nothing did: two waterfalls on a short trail, St. Mary and Virginia Falls. Or Ptarmigan Tunnel if anyone's still hungry.

    3.1 mi · 469 ft  /  or 10.7 mi · 2,362 ft
  8. Fri 30Drop-off

    Kalispell airport

    Pack up and head for FCA. Colin keeps going west to Oregon.

Every hike is optional. Trail numbers are from AllTrails, except the Highline (nps.gov). The sun sets around 9:30 PM, with nearly 17 hours of daylight.

Where we sleep

Many Glacier

Fri–Mon · 4 nights · east side, ~4,900 ft

The valley that gave the park its reputation. Iceberg Lake and Grinnell start here.

Avalanche (or Apgar)

Tue–Thu · 3 nights · west side

Cedar forest at the foot of the Sun Road. Avalanche Lake starts at camp.

Nothing is booked yet. These sites open six months ahead, Many Glacier on January 23 and Avalanche on January 27, and they go the same day. If one misses, there are backups (St. Mary on the east side, Apgar on the west), and the week still happens.

Glacier allows two tents per site. Colin has his, so you two share one tent.

Know this before you go

  • Grizzly country. Everyone carries bear spray on their body, not in the pack. You can't fly with it, so buy it in Bozeman before pickup. Food and anything scented, toothpaste included, goes in the bear box every night.
  • Two big days. Grinnell and the Highline are each over 11 miles. If you can comfortably walk 10 miles with some climbing now, you'll be fine. If not, start walking.
  • The Highline ledge is exposed. It isn't climbing and kids do it, but if heights get to you, the turnaround is at the cable and there's no shame in it.
  • Early starts. Iceberg, Grinnell and the Highline all start around 5–6 AM. The lots fill by 7.
  • Cold mornings. Roughly 76°F days and 46°F nights at Many Glacier (an estimate from nearby weather normals). The passes run 15°F colder and windy.
  • Some 2027 rules aren't announced yet, including the Highline shuttle and Logan Pass parking. Both ran as trials in 2026. There's a backup hike if the shuttle doesn't run.

What to bring

  • Soft bags only. Three people and three people's camping gear go in one Subaru Legacy. A duffel squashes; a hard suitcase doesn't.
  • One tent for the two of you, and a sleeping bag and pad each. Bags rated around 30–40°F are plenty.
  • Broken-in hiking shoes, a daypack that carries 2 liters of water, a rain shell, a warm layer and a hat.
  • A headlamp, sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • Colin brings the stove, cooler and kitchen.
