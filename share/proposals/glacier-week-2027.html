Fishing Swiftcurrent

Thu · the rules

Thursday afternoon, after Iceberg Lake, we fish Swiftcurrent Lake from the shore a few minutes from camp. The goal is one trout cooked for dinner. Glacier's lakes are cold and low in food, so the fish run small and nothing is guaranteed. Treat a fish as a bonus.

  • No license needed inside the park.
  • One of you brings a rod. A spinning rod that breaks down or telescopes, packed in your checked bag, plus line and a few small spinners and hooks. The park allows one rod per person, so we take turns.
  • No lead of any kind. No lead sinkers, jigs or lures. Steel, tin or tungsten instead. Check the packaging.
  • Bait is allowed here. Worms are legal in the Many Glacier valley, which most of the park doesn't allow. Worth grabbing in Kalispell if a shop has them (not checked).
  • Which fish we can keep: brook trout (red or orange spots with blue rings around them) and rainbow trout. Everything else goes back: bull trout, lake trout, and any cutthroat (a red slash under the jaw). The park's rule if you're not sure what it is: let it go.
  • Bear country rules for a kept fish: guts go in a garbage can, the skin stays on, and everything that smells of fish goes in the bear box after dinner.

Rules from Glacier's fishing page (nps.gov, updated June 17, 2026). Check the 2027 version before you buy tackle.

Know this before you go

  • Grizzly country. Everyone carries bear spray on their body, not in the pack. You can't fly with it, so it gets bought in Kalispell on pickup day. Food and anything scented, toothpaste included, goes in the bear box every night.
  • Two big days. Grinnell and the Highline are each over 11 miles. If you can comfortably walk 10 miles with some climbing now, you'll be fine. If not, start walking.
  • The Highline ledge is exposed. It isn't climbing and kids do it, but if heights get to you, the turnaround is at the cable and there's no shame in it.
  • Early starts. Iceberg, Grinnell and the Highline all start around 5–6 AM. The lots fill by 7.
  • Cold mornings. Roughly 76°F days and 46°F nights at Many Glacier (an estimate from nearby weather normals). The passes run 15°F colder and windy.
  • Some 2027 rules aren't announced yet, including the Highline shuttle and Logan Pass parking. Both ran as trials in 2026. There's a backup hike if the shuttle doesn't run.
