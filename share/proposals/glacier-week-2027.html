Know this before you go

  • Grizzly country. Everyone carries bear spray on their body, not in the pack. You can't fly with it, so it gets bought in Kalispell on pickup day. Food and anything scented, toothpaste included, goes in the bear box every night.
  • Two big days. Grinnell and the Highline are each over 11 miles. If you can comfortably walk 10 miles with some climbing now, you'll be fine. If not, start walking.
  • The Highline ledge is exposed. It isn't climbing and kids do it, but if heights get to you, the turnaround is at the cable and there's no shame in it.
  • Early starts. Iceberg, Grinnell and the Highline all start around 5–6 AM. The lots fill by 7.
  • Cold mornings. Roughly 76°F days and 46°F nights at Many Glacier (an estimate from nearby weather normals). The passes run 15°F colder and windy.
  • Some 2027 rules aren't announced yet, including the Highline shuttle and Logan Pass parking. Both ran as trials in 2026. There's a backup hike if the shuttle doesn't run.

What to bring

  • Soft bags only. Three people and three people's camping gear go in one Subaru Legacy. A duffel squashes; a hard suitcase doesn't.
  • One tent for the two of you, and a sleeping bag and pad each. Bags rated around 30–40°F are plenty.
  • Broken-in hiking shoes, a daypack that carries 2 liters of water, a rain shell, a warm layer and a hat.
  • A headlamp, sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • Colin brings the stove, cooler and kitchen.
