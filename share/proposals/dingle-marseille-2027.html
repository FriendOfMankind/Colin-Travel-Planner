The two halves in one minute

Dingle
A harbour town at the end of a peninsula in County Kerry, as far west as Ireland goes. Around Slea Head there are stone beehive huts, an Iron Age fort on a cliff edge and a 1,200-year-old church built without mortar.
Clare and the Aran Islands
The Cliffs of Moher, then a ferry from Doolin to Inis Mór: stone walls, bikes, and Dún Aonghasa, a prehistoric fort right on the edge of a sea cliff. The Burren next door is bare limestone with a Neolithic tomb in the middle of it.
Skye
Mountains, sea lochs and single-track roads. The Old Man of Storr and the Quiraing are landslips that left pinnacles and cliffs behind. Dunvegan Castle is the MacLeod seat. The Fairy Pools are clear blue pools under the Cuillin.
May
Mild and changeable in both countries, with long evenings. Bring a real rain jacket and layers. Ireland and Scotland are both 5 hours ahead of Cleveland.

Good to know

One driver, short days

Colin drives everything, on the left, and no day has more than about four hours at the wheel. Skye's roads are often one lane with passing places, so they're slower than the map says.

Hiking

The Storr, the Quiraing and the Lost Valley are each 3–4 miles on rough, often wet path. Bring boots or real trail shoes and a waterproof jacket.

Weather

Plan for rain any day and sun some of them. The spare Kerry day exists for that, and every Skye day can swap with another.

Packing

Soft bags, not hard suitcases: light enough to carry onto two coaches on day two, and squashable enough that four people's bags fit in one car.
