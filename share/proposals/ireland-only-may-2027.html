The plan · May 2027

Ireland, May 9–22

Same dates, same flights from Cleveland, no Scotland. Four nights with Stick's grandparents in Dingle, then one car up the west coast: Killarney and a boat to Skellig Michael, three nights in Doolin for the Cliffs of Moher, the Aran Islands and the Burren, two nights in Galway with a day in Connemara, and the nonstop home on Saturday, May 22.

Colin's pitch for switching: we were going to get a rushed Ireland and a rushed Scotland. This is one country done properly, with boats, bikes, kayaks and a cave in place of two flights and a second rental car.

Sun May 9 · evening

Overnight to Dublin

Cleveland → Dublin nonstop · sleep on the plane

Sleep: on the plane
Mon May 10

Across Ireland to Dingle

Land ~8:45 · coach to Killarney · local bus to Dingle by 7:30

  • No driving on the day we land. The 11:00 coach from Dublin Airport, then the 6 PM local bus out to Dingle. Dinner and an early night, or Neligan's if anyone is still standing: Monday is its set-dancing night with live music.
Sleep: with family in Dingle (night 1 of 4)
Tue May 11

Slea Head and the Great Blasket

Local bus to the end of Ireland · a boat to an abandoned island village

  • The 9 AM bus to Dunquin, then the boat out to Great Blasket Island (about €60 each): an abandoned village, open to wander.
  • If the boat isn't running: the cliff path at Dunquin Pier, the Blasket Centre and the 1,200-year-old Gallarus Oratory.
Sleep: Dingle (night 2 of 4)
Wed May 12

Dingle from the water

Sea kayaks into the harbour caves · Eask Tower · fish and chips

  • Half a day of guided sea kayaking around the harbour and into its sea caves (about €60–65 each, wetsuits included). Then the steep walk up to Eask Tower above the bay, or nothing at all.
  • Evening: Dingle's music pubs. O'Sullivan's Courthouse and John Benny's for traditional sessions, Foxy John's and Dick Mack's for the crowd. Music starts after 9.
Sleep: Dingle (night 3 of 4)
Thu May 13

The spare Kerry day

Rain backup · or Inch Beach and a castle ruin · farewell dinner

  • Whatever the weather pushed off moves here. If nothing did: Inch, a three-mile sand spit, Minard Castle on its boulder beach, and the pub an Antarctic explorer opened. Then a farewell dinner with the family.
Sleep: Dingle (night 4 of 4)
Fri May 14

The car, and the Gap of Dunloe

Pick up the car at Kerry Airport · 20 minutes to Killarney · a walk through a mountain pass

  • A lift from the family to Kerry Airport (still to confirm) and the car. A very short first drive to Killarney, then the Gap of Dunloe on foot: a narrow mountain pass.
  • Evening in Killarney, a bigger and livelier town: Courtney's, or the Dunloe Lodge for live music.
Sleep: The Black Sheep Hostel, Killarney (night 1 of 2)
Sat May 15

Skellig Michael

A boat to a sea rock · about 600 stone steps · an early-medieval monastery

  • An early drive to Portmagee and the boat out to Skellig Michael: stone beehive huts that monks built on top of a rock out in the Atlantic. About €140–145 each.
  • Heads upLandings are limited to 180 people a day and cancelled when there's swell. If ours is cancelled, we walk the Kerry Cliffs and go looking for the 385-million-year-old footprints on Valentia Island instead.
Sleep: The Black Sheep Hostel (night 2 of 2)
Clare and Galway · May 16–22
Sun May 16

Over the Shannon to Doolin

A car ferry across the river · the Cliffs of Moher at sunset

  • About three hours with the 20-minute Tarbert ferry in the middle. The Cliffs of Moher in the evening, once the tour coaches have gone.
  • Doolin has three music pubs, Gus O'Connor's, McGann's and McDermott's, with free sessions most nights from about 9. Three nights, one each.
  • MaybeClare play Cork at hurling in Ennis this spring, and Ennis is on today's road. The date isn't published yet. If it's this Sunday, we go.
Sleep: Doolin Court, our own townhouse (night 1 of 3)
Mon May 17

Inis Mór

35-minute ferry · bikes · a stone fort on a cliff edge

  • The morning boat from Doolin, bikes from the pier, and out to Dún Aonghasa, a prehistoric fort on the edge of a sheer sea cliff. Back on the afternoon boat.
  • Heads upThere's no railing at the cliff edge. We stay well back, especially in wind.
Sleep: Doolin Court (night 2 of 3)
Tue May 18

The Burren, and the O'Dea castle

A hike over bare limestone · the O'Dee family's tower house · a cave

  • A 3-hour loop up Mullaghmore in Burren National Park, a hill of folded bare limestone. Free.
  • Dysert O'Dea Castle, the O'Dea clan's 15th-century tower. Colin and Finn's mom's side is O'Dee. It's due to reopen in May 2027; the date isn't posted yet.
  • If there's time: the Poulnabrone tomb, and Doolin Cave, with one of the world's largest stalactites (from €19.50).
Sleep: Doolin Court (night 3 of 3)
Wed May 19

Galway

An hour and a half up the coast · the city on foot · a night out

  • A short drive, past Dunguaire Castle on the bay at Kinvara.
  • A 2.5-mile walk through the city: the cathedral, the Latin Quarter's pedestrian streets, the Spanish Arch, across to the Claddagh and out along the bay. Add Salthill's promenade if the legs want more.
  • Fish and chips at McDonagh's on Quay Street, then Taaffes or Tigh Neachtain for music.
Sleep: Kinlay Eyre Square, Galway (night 1 of 2)
Thu May 20

Connemara

Diamond Hill · a fjord · an island you walk to at low tide

  • Diamond Hill: a 4.6-mile loop with about 1,240 feet of climbing and a view over the whole coast. Then Killary Fjord, or Omey Island across the sand if the tide is out.
Sleep: Kinlay Eyre Square (night 2 of 2)
Fri May 21

Back to Dublin

A monastery on the Shannon · return the car · airport hotel

  • About two and a half hours of motorway, with a stop at Clonmacnoise, a ruined monastery on the river (€8). Return the car at Dublin Airport and sleep there.
Sleep: the Maldron, Dublin Airport
Sat May 22

Home

A slow morning · Dublin → Cleveland nonstop

  • Aer Lingus nonstop at 4:05 PM, landing in Cleveland at 7:10 PM. US customs is done in Dublin before boarding, and Stick has Sunday to spare before work.

Evenings, and a match

Every stop

Music in the pubs

Dingle, Killarney, Doolin and Galway all have pubs with live traditional music most nights, usually from about 9, and it's free to walk in. Everyone on this trip is over 18, which is the age that matters in Ireland.

If the dates line up

A hurling or football match

Hurling is Clare's game and Gaelic football is Kerry's. The 2027 Munster hurling championship has Clare at home to Cork in Ennis, date not yet published. Kerry's footballers play in Killarney. We'll check the fixtures when they come out and go to whichever falls on our route.

Good to know

One driver, short days

Colin drives everything, on the left, in one automatic. Most days are under two hours at the wheel; the Skellig day and the last day are the long ones, at about three.

Boats

Skellig Michael, the Great Blasket and Inis Mór all depend on the sea. Each has a plan for the day it doesn't sail.

Weather

Plan for rain any day and sun some of them. Bring a real rain jacket, layers, and boots or trail shoes.

Jake

Staying in Ireland makes this easier from Belfast. Meet us off the plane in Dublin on May 10, or join wherever suits: Galway on the 19th is the simplest. Tell Colin which.
