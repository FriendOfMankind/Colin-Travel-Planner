The deal

Two seats

Up to two people at a time. The rest of the car is camping gear.

Your tent, Colin's site

Bring a tent, a pad and a warm bag. You pitch on Colin's campsite. A few nights are motels and one is a ferry.

Split everything

Gas, campsites, park passes, ferries and shared groceries, split evenly for the days you're along. Your flights are yours.

Share the wheel

Some days are 8–11 hours of road. You can take driving shifts.

Already coming on a friends' week?

You can skip your flight home and ride back with Colin. From St. John's (June 19): Ferryland, the Mistaken Point fossils, the gannets at Cape St. Mary's, an overnight ferry, then three days to Ohio. From Portland (August 12): four days of interstate to Ohio, with driving shifts. Either way you save a flight and split the gas.

Know this before you say yes

The Rockies

  • Altitude. Camps go up to about 10,500 ft in the Snowy Range and above 9,500 ft on the Beartooth Plateau.
  • Bears. Grizzly country from the Beartooths north. Carry bear spray. You can't fly with it, so buy it out there.
  • Big hiking days. Medicine Bow Peak tops out at 12,013 ft, and Lost Twin Lakes in the Bighorns is 11 miles. Every hike is optional.
  • Yellowstone's Slough Creek has 16 sites that go in minutes. If it misses, that night moves.

Newfoundland

  • You need a passport, for Canada and Labrador.
  • It's cold. Normal lows are 35–41°F on the island in early June, with highs in the 40s and 50s. The bigger problem is days of fog and drizzle.
  • Moose on the road at dusk, and sunset isn't until 8:45–9:30 PM.
  • Ferries. Four crossings, each charging a fare per passenger. The 2026 adult fare was roughly $60 one way on the big ferry (a search result, not checked on Marine Atlantic's site).

Deadlines

Rockies: tell Colin by January 10

The campground windows for the open stretch run January 13 (Vedauwoo) to January 23 (Glacier's west side), with Slough Creek on January 21. They sell out the day they open.

Newfoundland: tell Colin by January 31

Acadia opens December 1, which only matters if you're riding from the start. Parks Canada opened its campgrounds in early February in 2026, and the Newfoundland provincial parks open in mid-April.

The airports

From Cleveland, plan on one connection to any of these. That wasn't checked airport by airport, and no 2027 fares exist yet.

  • Cody (COD). United from Denver several times a day. In summer 2026 there was also one Chicago flight a week, in on Fridays and out on Saturdays (Cody Yellowstone, Jan 2026). 2027 isn't announced.
  • Bozeman (BZN). Montana's busiest, with about two dozen nonstops including Chicago and Denver, plus Detroit in summer (bozemanairport.com).
  • Kalispell (FCA). Denver, Salt Lake City, Seattle and Minneapolis, plus Chicago in summer (iflyglacier.com; nonstopscout.com). The last place to get off, on July 25.
  • Denver (DEN). Easy to fly into, but off the route. See the table.
  • Bangor (BGR). Chicago in summer, Newark, Detroit and others (nonstopscout.com, 2026).
  • Halifax (YHZ). Atlantic Canada's main airport. Its routes weren't checked for this page.
  • Deer Lake (YDF). Air Canada about 12 nonstops a week from Toronto, Porter about 7 a week from June to September, and a daily Halifax flight (search results, Oct 2026).
  • St. John's (YYT). Through Toronto or Halifax. One-way fares to Cleveland ran about $295–436 in October 2026 (search results; current fares, not 2027).
