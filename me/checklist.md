# Universal checklist

- [ ] Offline Google Maps regions downloaded for the whole route — before leaving home
- [ ] Trail maps downloaded separately (AllTrails/Gaia) — Google Maps offline has no trails
- [ ] GPX loaded for any route with poor blazing or route-finding
- [ ] Starlink sky view tested on arrival day at every forested campground
- [ ] Trip plan texted home, with the date and time of every no-signal segment
- [ ] Hard turnaround time set for the biggest day, and a bail-out named before the forest road
- [ ] Fire and burn ban status checked for every state on the route
- [ ] Bear food storage sorted where required
- [ ] Block ice — not cubes — on the resupply list
- [ ] Fuel canisters sourced at the destination on any fly-in trip
- [ ] Spare tire pressure checked before any gravel road
- [ ] Permits and reservations printed on paper, plus photo ID
- [ ] Road conditions re-confirmed by phone the morning of departure

## Theft and scams: city trips and abroad

Adopted 2026-10-30 from general advice Claude gave, not from a source or a past trip *(stated 2026-10-30)*. Trip-specific scams (like Marseille Saint-Charles) live in that trip's `trip.md`, not here.

- [ ] Travel insurance quoted before anyone books, and its cost on the trip's budget table
- [ ] Find My on for every phone; passport photos saved to the cloud and shared with the group
- [ ] Cards split: each person carries one and leaves a backup in the lodging
- [ ] A decoy wallet: an old wallet, ~€10 or $10 and an expired card
- [ ] Waterproof phone pouch on a lanyard for swim days

On the ground:
- **Swim in shifts.** One person stays with the bags; bags go where the swimmers can see them, not beside the path.
- **Passports stay locked in the lodging.** Beach and hike days carry one card, a little cash and a phone.
- **ATMs inside or attached to a bank.** At a card machine, always pay in the local currency.
- **Phones off café tables at the terrace edge.**
- **Anyone who approaches first wants something.** Petitions, bracelets, "dropped ring", helpers at ticket machines: walk away without explaining.
- **Official taxi ranks or the airport shuttle only.**
- **In crowds** (ports, metros, markets): wallet in a front pocket, pack zipped and worn on the front.
- **Close ground-floor windows** when leaving the lodging.
