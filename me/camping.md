# Camping style

All stated in the 2026-09-28 interview unless marked otherwise.

## Campsites

- **Favourites: developed campgrounds with showers, and scenic sites where the view beats the facilities.** *(stated 2026-09-28)*
- Not chosen: private/bike-farm character stays, or dispersed/primitive sites. Those aren't banned, but a no-facilities night needs a reason: a scenic payoff, or a region with nothing else.
- Showers daily if possible (`me/profile.md`). **Late at night is fine**, so a shower doesn't need a daytime block *(stated 2026-10-20)*.

## Evenings

- **Cook a real dinner, then early to bed.** *(stated 2026-09-28)* A campfire wasn't picked as part of the point.
- Up early, then an unhurried camp morning: warm up in the tent, hot breakfast, then hike (`me/principles.md` 4) *(stated 2026-10-29; was: "Mornings are slow")*. Sunset viewpoints over sunrise ones.

## Rain

- **Hike anyway when rain makes it better, or at least still enjoyable** (waterfalls especially). Otherwise it's a scenic drive or a restock day. *(stated 2026-09-28)*
- Not chosen: a tent day, or bailing to a motel.

## Cold

- **Warm enough down to ~30°F** with the Siesta 20 + liner: the 29°F freeze-warning night at Arrowhead (Oct 17) and the mid-30s FS 210 nights were all fine *(stated 2026-10-27, first appalachians-2026 retro)*. (was: "Comfortable down to the 40s… below that is untested", stated 2026-09-28)
- Below ~30°F is untested. The 29°F figure is the NWS forecast low for Fayetteville, not a thermometer at the tent.
