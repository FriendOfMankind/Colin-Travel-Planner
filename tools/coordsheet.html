How to place one (about 30 seconds)
  1. Tap Find in Maps. Check it's the right spot: the trailhead lot, not the summit; the campground entrance, not the town. Satellite view helps.
  2. Long-press the exact spot. Tap the coordinates that appear to copy them.
  3. Paste here. Clean coordinates save on paste. Anything you type after them, like (the lot), is kept as a note.
A Google Maps link with !3d…!4d… in it works too. Short share links (maps.app.goo.gl) don't, because the page can't open them. If it isn't one spot (a whole harbour, a road), tap Not a pin.
