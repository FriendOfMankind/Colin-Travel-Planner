Photo Ledger

How this works
  1. Find pictures opens an image search for that shot in a new tab.
  2. On a computer: copy the image, come back, click the shot's box and paste. Or drag the image onto the box.
  3. On a phone: save the image, then tap the box and pick it from your photos.
  4. It's saved the moment the thumbnail appears. Tell Claude "pull my pictures" and the slideshow gets them.
