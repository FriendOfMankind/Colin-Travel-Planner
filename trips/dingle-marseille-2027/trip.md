# Dingle & Marseille

> ⚠️ Two transfer days decide whether this trip works, and neither is a verified chain yet. **May 11:** Dublin Airport → Dingle by public transport is a 7½-hour planning block, not a timetable. **May 15:** a family lift to Killarney for the 09:00 FlightLink, 4 h to Dublin Airport, then a Ryanair flight at 15:45 on a separate ticket, so a late bus has no protection. The fallback is a Dublin airport hotel on May 14, which costs a Dingle evening. Everything in Marseille is easy by comparison.

## Overview

Fly overnight from Cleveland, cross Ireland to Dingle the day you land, then four nights and three full days with family. On May 15 get to Dublin Airport and fly Ryanair to Marseille. One Marseille base for seven nights: the old town and Mucem, Notre-Dame de la Garde and the Corniche, the Calanques from Luminy, Cassis and Aix by train, and one real rest day. Home on May 22 via Heathrow and Chicago, landing at 22:02.

### Dates

May 10–22, 2027 (target). 13 days, 12 nights. The return was May 20, then pushed to May 22 for more Dingle time. **Landing May 22 at 22:02 leaves no turnaround before Newfoundland's May 23 start, so Newfoundland moves** *(Colin, 2026-10-30)*. How far it moves is an open question in `trips/newfoundland-2027/trip.md`.

### Group

**Everyone flies from Cleveland together on the same flights** *(stated 2026-10-30)*. Two adults for certain, **a third may join for the whole trip** *(handoff, 2026-10-29)*. Willing to share a double bed or take a sofa bed to save money. The repo's earlier note (friend out of school May 1, already in Kerry, flight paid by his family) is superseded by this; see `log.md`.

### Family in Dingle

Family is in Dingle *(handoff, 2026-10-29)*. **Assumed, not confirmed:** free beds for 4 nights and a short lift on the morning of May 15. Colin's words for the lift: "maybe a short ride at a reasonable hour." Nobody is assumed to drive to Dublin or Cork, chauffeur a sightseeing day, or leave before dawn.

### Marseille or Athens

**Marseille for now** *(stated 2026-10-30: "I'm leaning that, but remember Athens, nothing is set")*. Athens, with Delphi and Meteora, stays researched in `wishlist/ireland-may-2027.md`. Marseille's edge is the Calanques in their best hiking month and a one-hour flight from Dublin. Athens' edge is the oldest ruins and Meteora.

### Whit Monday

**May 17 is Whit Monday, a French public holiday** *(source: service-public.gouv.fr)*. Buses run on holiday timetables and some restaurants close. Day 8 is outdoors on purpose.

### Clocks

Cleveland is UTC−4, Dublin UTC+1, Marseille UTC+2 (all on summer time in May). Every time on this page is local to where you stand.
