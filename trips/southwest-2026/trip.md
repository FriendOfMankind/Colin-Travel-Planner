# The Ultimate American Southwest Adventure

## Overview

### Source

Imported 2026-10-06 from Colin's own trip site (`data.js`, the later plan with bookings) and the earlier pitch document (`Southwest_Trip_Plan_2026_-_Final_Version.docx`). This is **the plan**, not a record. What actually happened is in `log.md`. Confirmation numbers and prices are left out on purpose: the trip is over and they are dead.

### Group

5 people ("Brocock"): Colin, Luke, Aiden and Stick from Cleveland; Ben flew into Las Vegas on day 10. Colin was the main driver. Vehicle: Toyota Sienna, van-camp setup.
