### 2026-10-30 · Winter day trips: beginner Nordic and Mayflower Gulch
From a handoff Colin built in another AI chat. Its facts are its own and unverified unless marked ✅.
- ⚠️ **The handoff says the December dates aren't set. This file says Dec 25–30, confirmed 2026-09-30.** This file wins unless Colin says otherwise. Whether the group skis at all, and at what level, isn't recorded anywhere.
- **Its ranking:** (1) Mayflower Gulch, the scenic priority. (2) The Frisco–Breckenridge rec path, point to point. (3) Frisco Nordic Center, to learn and rent. (4) Boreas Pass Road, later or guided. (5) Miners Creek Road. (6) Gold Run Nordic Center. (7) Mount Royal, a winter hike, not a ski. (8) Vail Pass → Frisco, rejected for beginners.
- **Its sequence:** day one is a lesson at Frisco Nordic plus 2–4 mi of groomed track. Day two is the rec path, point to point, with a pickup. Day three is a Mayflower Gulch snowshoe.
- ✅ **The rec path is groomed on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays:** 8 mi from the Frisco Nordic Center to the Breckenridge Recreation Center. It's free, but where it crosses the Frisco Nordic trails you need a day pass to leave it *(source: townoffrisco.com, Winter Rec Path Uses, 2026-10-30)*. In Dec 25–30 the grooming days are **Fri Dec 25, Mon Dec 28 and Wed Dec 30**, so **Mon Dec 28 or Tue Dec 29** is the fresh-corduroy day for the point-to-point.
- ✅ **Frisco Nordic Center: projected opening Thu Nov 26, 2026,** weather dependent. 28–30 km of ski trail and 8 km of snowshoe trail. Classic, skate and snowshoe rentals *(source: townoffrisco.com, 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-30)*.
- ✅ **Lessons are private only**, no group classes. A full-day trail pass is included, rentals aren't *(source: townoffrisco.com, Nordic Ski Lessons, 2026-10-30)*. Lesson prices weren't in the snippet: TBD.
- 📋 Day passes at Gold Run, Breckenridge Nordic and Frisco Nordic are valid at all three *(source: breckenridgerecreation.com, 2026-10-30)*.
- ⚠️ Rental ~$30/day ($35 Dec 17 – Jan 4) and trail pass ~$35: the handoff's figures, not checked.
- ⚠️ **Mayflower Gulch:** ~3–4 mi round trip, ~550–600 ft, 15–20 min from Frisco on CO-91, toward the Boston Mine cabins. **Avalanche terrain around the basin, so the cabins aren't a safe default.** Snowshoe it. A ski descent only with a guide. Check CAIC (avalanche.state.co.us) the morning of. All of this is the handoff's, not checked.
- Fits principle 10 in `me/principles.md` (explorable, unrestored mining ruins in a big basin), and sunset is ~4:40–4:50 PM, so it's a midday outing. Being at 9,100 ft the first day off a plane counts.
