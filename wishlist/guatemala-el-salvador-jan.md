# Fuego & Surf

*Guatemala + El Salvador with friends — a night facing an erupting volcano, then surf towns and crater lakes*

## Why

Colin asked what's worth doing in Central or South America with the January gap, possibly skipping the Adirondacks *(stated 2026-11-03)*. What he picked: **~10 days, with friends (the Adirondacks crew, none committed yet), volcanoes + jungle, beach/surf time for them, the crew can hike hard, budget is bang-for-buck, nobody speaks Spanish, nobody is 25+** *(stated 2026-11-03)*. Hard hiking is welcome but not required *(stated 2026-11-03)*.

This is the version with the one thing nothing else on the list has: **a night at Acatenango's camp facing Fuego erupting**. Then a shuttle to El Salvador's surf coast, which has its own volcano day hikes and is a Level 1 country.

**Horizon:** a 10-day fly trip fits two weeks of PTO, so on the `me/calendar.md` test it's not now-or-never. What *is* now-only is having a free January at all, and a crew of students with the same gap.

**Rules it bends, on purpose:**
- **Principle 1 (car camping only):** Acatenango is a guided overnight hike-in. Colin's call: "maybe, if it's the one exception" *(stated 2026-11-03)*. Write the reason in the plan if it becomes a trip.
- **No car.** Shuttles between towns, tours to trailheads. Driving Guatemala isn't advised (below); under 25 for rentals everywhere.

## The shape (sketch, not a plan)

| Leg | Nights | What |
|---|---|---|
| Fly CLE → Guatemala City, shuttle to Antigua | 3–4 | Acclimatize (camp is ~2,000 m above Antigua); Acatenango overnight; Pacaya as a short warm-up option |

## Research notes

### 2026-11-03 · First pass: safety, Acatenango, transport, flights
- ✅ **Guatemala: Level 3, Reconsider travel** (crime, terrorism), issued 2026-03-12. Level 4 Do Not Travel: San Marcos and Huehuetenango departments, Zone 18 of Guatemala City, Villa Nueva. US government staff *may* visit Antigua, Lake Atitlán, Tikal and the Pacific coast, but not drive outside Guatemala City at night, use white taxis or chicken buses. Tourist police (DISETUR/ASISTUR) in Antigua and Atitlán: dial 1500 *(source: travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/guatemala.html, 2026-11-03)*
- ✅ **El Salvador: Level 1, Exercise normal precautions**, issued 2026-06-25 *(source: travel.state.gov advisories list, 2026-11-03)*
- ⚠️ Whether the Antigua → El Tunco shuttle routes near Villa Nueva or Zone 18: not checked
- 📋 **Acatenango:** camp ~3,600 m; normal nights 2–5 °C, below freezing on many nights Dec–Feb. Six hikers died of hypothermia in a Jan 2017 cold snap (−6 °C, wind). Hiking on to Fuego is **banned** since 2026-04-01 (municipality notice). The park closes at short notice in eruptive phases: shut Aug 4–7, 2026; also halted Mar and Jun 2025 *(source: exploreacatenango.com/en/blog/is-acatenango-safe, an operator, dated 2026-10-24)*
- 📋 Acatenango 2-day tours on GetYourGuide list from ~€80–103 *(source: getyourguide.com listings, search snippets, 2026-11-03)*. Not a quote; what's included varies
- ⚠️ Colin is warm to ~30°F in his own Siesta 20 + liner (`me/camping.md`), but tours supply the bag. Its rating is the question, not his tolerance
- 📋 **Antigua → El Tunco/El Zonte shuttle:** $40–55 pp, 5.5–7.5 h, book 24–48 h ahead; ~$1 border fee in cash *(sources: livinginguatemala.com, 2026-07; shuttlenation.com; roneeyshuttle.com; tropicanahostel.com, search snippets, 2026-11-03)*
- 📋 **Santa Ana (Ilamatepec):** 2,381 m, 6.9 km round trip, 471 m gain; guide mandatory, police escort; doesn't run in rain; tours from the surf towns pick up 05:00–05:30, ~€66–141 *(source: volcano.travel/el-salvador/santa-ana-volcano, data "verified 2026-08-12", search snippet)*. Moderate, not hard
- 📋 **Izalco:** 1,950 m, ~45° slope, rated 6/10, same park complex, $3 foreigners *(source: elsalvador.travel/destination/izalco-volcano, search snippet)*. The harder one
- 📋 **Conchagua:** summit-deck camping for the Gulf of Fonseca sunrise; 4×4 or ~2 h hike from the base; ~$40 pp packages from La Unión *(source: onmetravel.com/conchagua-volcano, 2026-06, snippet)*. 3–4.5 h from San Salvador, so it's the all-El-Salvador fallback, not a fit with Antigua in 10 days
- 📋 San Miguel (Chaparrastique): authorities steer hikers to Santa Ana / Cerro Verde instead *(source: elsalvador.com, 2026-07, snippet)*
- 📋 **Flights:** January is the cheapest month from CLE to Guatemala City (avg ~$334 round trip) and to San Salvador (avg ~$372–424) *(sources: momondo, KAYAK route pages, Oct 2026, snippets)*. Aggregator averages, not quotes; Spirit and Frontier fly both cities from CLE
- 📋 El Tunco beginner surf lessons are listed on GetYourGuide from ~$85 *(snippet, 2026-11-03)*. Not checked against local shops, which are likely cheaper ⚠️
