# Sleeping Bear Dunes

*Ride out of camp, swim at the end*

## Why

The closest of the full bike trips, and the swimming one. 📋 The Heritage Trail runs about 20 miles, mostly paved, with a packed-gravel stretch through the Port Oneida historic district *(source: friendsofsleepingbear.org, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. 📋 A loop runs through D.H. Day Campground, so you ride straight out of camp *(source: nps.gov/slbe, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. The catch: polished and busy, the most likely of the four to feel "interpretive" (the Blue Heron lesson in `trips/kentucky-2026/log.md`).
