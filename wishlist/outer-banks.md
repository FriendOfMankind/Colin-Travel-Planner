# Outer Banks Bodyboard Week

*Dune-top camp, warm Atlantic, small waves over a sandbar*

## Why

The boogie-board beach trip, drivable from Avon and with no passport. Warm water, beginner-size waves breaking well offshore over a gradual sandbar (the opposite of Hawaii's famous neck-breaking shorebreaks), and NPS campgrounds a short walk from the ocean. Cheapest beach trip on the list.

The scenery is flat: dunes, lighthouses, sunsets over Pamlico Sound. Colin's fine with that: "I'm cool with flat scenery as long as is made up for by like... the ocean" *(stated 2026-11-12)*.

**When:** a PTO trip, after full-time work starts *(Colin, 2026-11-12)*. It passes the horizon test as a keeper: about 5–6 days by car, so a weekend plus two PTO days does it forever. Summer 2027 was considered and passed over: the gaps around Newfoundland (ends Jun 24) and Glacier (Jul 12 – Aug 16) are either recovery time between two huge trips or the burnout week before work starts.

Picked over Costa Rica for the beach itch: Costa Rica stays dropped for January (`wishlist/costa-rica-jan.md`, 2026-11-08), and solo it fails bang-for-buck.

## Next

Nothing for 2027. When a PTO summer comes: pick the month (August for the warmest water, accepting hurricane risk; June–July for calmer odds), then a campground. Frisco is the lead.

### 2026-11-12 · First pass: water, waves, campgrounds
- 📋 Water: "June through early October the water holds 70°F or better, peaking around 78-82 in August"; May and late October are wetsuit-optional shoulder months *(source: obxmaps.com/outer-banks-water-temperature, search snippet, 2026-11-12)*. July averages ~80.4°F *(source: villagerealtyobx.com, search snippet, 2026-11-12)*. Winter is far too cold for this (recalled), so it's a summer-only idea
- ✅ Waves: "In Frisco and Hatteras, some surfers head to the 4WD accessible beaches for decent waves that are both on the smaller side and break well past a gradual sand bar. These areas are ideal for beginners" *(source: outerbanks.com/surfing.html, 2026-11-12)*
- ✅ Ocracoke: a public lifeguarded beach about 2 miles south of the village is the beginner spot; the island "is a must after a summer storm, hurricane or Nor'easter" *(source: outerbanks.com/surfing.html, 2026-11-12)*
- ✅ Corolla and Carova: small summer swells, warm water *(source: outerbanks.com/surfing.html, 2026-11-12)*. ⚠️ Carova is 4WD-only beach driving (recalled), so it's out for the Subaru
- ⚠️ **The best empty beginner beaches near Frisco/Hatteras are 4WD-access** (above). The Subaru is AWD but low-clearance (`me/profile.md`, principle 2): plan on paved access and walk-overs, not beach driving. Beach driving needs an ORV permit, bought online only through recreation.gov *(source: nps.gov/caha hours page, search snippet, 2026-11-12)*
- 📋 Campgrounds: Oregon Inlet, Cape Point, Frisco and Ocracoke, all booked on recreation.gov. Oregon Inlet, Frisco and Ocracoke take reservations up to six months ahead; Cape Point is same-day only, 202 sites, 49 tent-only, open spring to late fall *(source: nps.gov/caha/planyourvisit/campgrounds.htm, search snippet, 2026-11-12)*
- 📋 **Showers: Oregon Inlet's are heated; Frisco, Cape Point and Ocracoke have unheated showers.** All have modern restrooms and potable water. 14-day limit per campground from Easter to the Sunday after Thanksgiving *(source: nps.gov/caha campgrounds page, search snippet, 2026-11-12)*
- 📋 Frisco: elevated dunes give some sites an ocean view, and dense vegetation gives more privacy than the other three; an ORV ramp sits at the campground entrance *(source: recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/251430 and nps.gov/caha, search snippets, 2026-11-12)*. The lead pick, per `me/camping.md` (scenic sites where the view beats the facilities)
- 📋 Prices: Ocracoke $28/night; Oregon Inlet $28–70/night *(source: recreation.gov/gateways/2609, search snippet, 2026-11-12)*. No entrance fee for the Seashore *(source: nps.gov/caha permits page, search snippet)*
- 📋 Cape Hatteras Lighthouse climbing is closed for a restoration project; the grounds stay open *(source: nps.gov/caha hours page, updated 2026-09-18, search snippet)*. Bodie Island's lighthouse climb runs in summer
- ⚠️ Not checked yet: drive time from Avon (guessed ~10–11 h to Hatteras Island), the Hatteras–Ocracoke ferry, NC 12 overwash closures, hurricane climatology by week (late summer is recalled as the peak), mosquitoes and biting flies at the campgrounds, and how the wind treats a tent on the dunes
- Rip currents: red-flag days happen, when swimmers are told to stay out *(source: tripadvisor.com OBX forum, search snippet, 2026-11-12)*. ⚠️ Bodyboard at lifeguarded beaches, and never alone in big surf
