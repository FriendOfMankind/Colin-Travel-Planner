# Owens Valley + Alabama Hills

*Alabama Hills granite under the Sierra wall*

## Why

🔻 **No longer a Maui bookend** *(decided 2026-10-05)*: Newfoundland leaves May 23, three days after Maui would have ended, so there was no room for a leg; Maui itself then moved to spring 2028 as a friends trip. This stands alone now, for a PTO year. *(was: subtitle "Maui bookend — the outbound leg")*

The Sierra escarpment is about **10,000 ft of relief in a few horizontal miles** — one of the great normal-fault fronts on Earth — and the 1872 Lone Pine earthquake scarp is walkable at the valley floor. Weathered granite at Alabama Hills, basalt scoured by the Owens River at Fossil Falls, and Manzanar for the ruins. **This is also the only piece of the Eastern Sierra that works in May**, so it doubles as a scouting trip for the committed summer version. ⚠️ Alabama Hills is now **designated sites only** (~50, first-come) plus a **free one-year permit**; no camping west of Movie Road. Tuttle Creek BLM — already on your Eastern Sierra list as the bad-weather fallback — is the drive-up base.
