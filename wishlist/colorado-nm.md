# Colorado National Monument

*The reliable Colorado option in May*

## Why

Sheer red rock canyons and monoliths off Rim Rock Drive, at 4,700–6,600 ft — **low enough to be snow-free in May when the Colorado mountains are still unusable**. Monument Canyon, Window Rock, Canyon Rim and the Alcove Nature Trail all leave from near the visitor center.

✅ **Saddlehorn Campground**: 79 sites, March–October, $22, reservable on recreation.gov, four miles from the Fruita entrance. **Loop C is tents and small campers only** — that is the one to want. The only established campground in the monument.

Pairs naturally with Black Canyon, 2.5 hours away, for a Western Slope loop out of Grand Junction.
