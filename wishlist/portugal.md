# Portugal by Car and Tent

*Drowned villages and granite in the north, west-facing cliffs in the south*

## Why

The best-value car-and-tent country found in the 2026-11-01 sweep, and Colin's pick of the European options *(stated 2026-11-01: "I like Portugal")*. Two halves that could be one 9–10 night trip or two:

- **North (the pick):** Peneda-Gerês, Portugal's only national park, for explorable ruins (`me/principles.md` 10), waterfalls and granite villages, with Porto as the city half.
- **Southwest:** the Costa Vicentina's west-facing Atlantic sunsets and the Fishermen's Trail, open September–June.

Horizon: ~10 days fits a PTO year, so it **keeps** (`me/calendar.md`). Considered for the May 2027 slot in place of Marseille on 2026-11-01; Colin kept Marseille.

## Research notes

### 2026-11-01 · First sweep
**North: Peneda-Gerês**
- 📋 **Parque Cerdeira**, beside the Vilarinho da Furna reservoir: open all year, pool, restaurant, mini-market, self-service laundry *(source: turismo.cm-terrasdebouro.pt, 2026-11-01)*
- 📋 **Vilarinho da Furna**, a village flooded for a dam in 1972; the ruins show in dry years at the end of summer or early autumn. Park at the dam, ~3 km on a dirt road *(source: vagamundos.pt, 2026-07; Wikipedia)*. Some years it never shows *(source: sornavelik.info, 2026-04)*
- 📋 **Old Juriz**, ~1.3 km SW of Pitões das Júnias, abandoned since the 1400s (perhaps the Black Death): walls and road traces in oak woods *(source: walkingpenedageres.pt)*. Plus the Santa Maria das Júnias monastery and waterfall at Pitões
- 📋 Granite grain stores at Soajo (24) and Lindoso (50, the most in Iberia), Lindoso castle, the Fafião wolf trap *(source: flagra.pt)*
- ⚠️ **Warning, from the source:** the best-known waterfalls (Tahiti, Arado, Portela do Homem) "have dangerous access with polished stone; there are casualties every year". Some pools partly dry in August *(source: flagra.pt)*
- 📋 A wildfire started in Gerês on Jul 26 2025 (see `wishlist/europe-atlantic-north-2027.md`)

**Southwest: Costa Vicentina + western Algarve**
- ✅ Fishermen's Trail: 13 stages, 226.5 km, walked **September to June**, 60–70% sand, no wild camping anywhere in the natural park *(source: rotavicentina.com, 2026-11-01)*. Stages run 12–23 km, mostly past the ~10 mi ceiling, so walk sections out and back. Salema → Luz is 12 km
- 📋 Campsites: Camping São Miguel, ~4 km from the Odeceixe and Carvalhal beaches *(source: campingsaomiguel.pt)*; Camping Villa Park, Zambujeira *(source: theoceanpreneur.com, 2025-12)*
- 📋 Seven Hanging Valleys: 6 km one way, +360 m, 12 km there and back; karst arches and sea caves; best Mar–May and Sep–Nov *(source: visitalgarve.pt; activealgarve.pt, 2026-04)*. Benagil cave swimming banned since 2024 (Atlantic North notes)

**Driving**
- 📋 Manual is the default in Portugal; automatics cost more and sell out in summer *(source: backpackingislife.com, 2026-05)*. November is the cheapest month to rent *(source: discovercars.com, 2026-03)*
