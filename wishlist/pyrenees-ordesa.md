## Research notes

### 2026-11-01 · First look
- 📋 Campsites in Torla: Camping Ordesa (180 pitches), Camping Río Ara (May–Oct), Camping Oto nearby *(source: wikivoyage, 2026-09)*
- 📋 Private cars are barred from the valley at Easter, May 1–3, Jun 19 – Sep 20 and several autumn weekends in 2026; the Torla shuttle is €6 return, first bus 6:00 *(source: hikepyrenees.co.uk; ordesabus.com)*. Valley capacity is 1,800 people at once *(source: ordesabus.com)*
- 📋 The valley-floor walk: 16 km, +450 m, 6–7 h *(source: hikepyrenees.co.uk)*, right at the ~10 mi ceiling
- 📋 No overnight camping anywhere in the Ordesa sector except the reserved Góriz area *(source: wikivoyage)*
