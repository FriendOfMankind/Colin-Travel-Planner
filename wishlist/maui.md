# Full Circle Maui

*Counterclockwise island loop — reef, lava field, rainforest and a 10,000 ft summit*

## Why

Every major Maui landscape in one loop: Kahului → Olowalu (west) → the South Maui lava coast → Paia → Hāna (east) → the Piʻilani Hwy across the south flank → the Haleakalā summit → OGG. No road is driven twice. It was built as an 8-day solo camping trip for May 13–20 2027, with Camp Olowalu ×2, Waiʻānapanapa ×3 and Hosmer Grove ×2 *(was a trips/ page; shelved 2026-10-05 for spring 2028 as a friends trip)*.
