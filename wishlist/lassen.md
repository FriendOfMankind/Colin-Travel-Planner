### 2026-10-10 · Manzanita Lake, as half of a Reno trip
- Context: Colin asked what fills August 2027. Possible pairing with the Eastern Sierra week (`wishlist/eastern-sierra.md`)
- 📋 **Manzanita Lake CG**: 179 sites, $26 with water on; the camper store has **pay showers, laundry, groceries and gas**, the only one in the park (nps.gov/lavo camping page, snippet, 2026-10-10). That fits showers-if-possible (`me/profile.md`)
- 📋 Reservations on recreation.gov, up to 6 months and at least 4 days ahead; after July 1 unreserved sites can be used first-come for a single night (parkrangerjohn.com, 2020, so old)
- 📋 The park highway is 30 mi and about an hour without stops (nps.gov/lavo, snippet)
