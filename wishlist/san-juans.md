### 2026-10-01 · Paid experiences: rent a Jeep for the Alpine Loop
- 📋 The loop is about 65 miles and takes 5–8+ hours *(source: San Juan Mountain Adventures Facebook group, search snippet, 2026-10-01)*
- 📋 Cinnamon Pass tops out at 12,600 ft and Engineer Pass at 12,800 ft; the western switchbacks between Animas Forks and Engineer's summit are narrow, rocky and exposed. From Lake City, 2WD reaches Capitol City and American Basin *(source: lakecity.com "Driving the Alpine Loop", search snippet, 2026-10-01)*
- 📋 Jeep Wrangler Rubicon rentals in Ouray reach Yankee Boy Basin, Imogene Pass and the Alpine Loop from Main Street *(source: coloradowestutvandjeeps.com, search snippet, 2026-10-01)*. Price TBD
- 📋 Renters report doing the full loop in a rented 4x4 *(source: tripadvisor, reddit r/ColoradoOffroad, search snippets, 2026-10-01)*
