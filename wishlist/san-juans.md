### 2026-10-01 · Paid experiences: rent a Jeep for the Alpine Loop
- 📋 The loop is about 65 miles and takes 5–8+ hours *(source: San Juan Mountain Adventures Facebook group, search snippet, 2026-10-01)*
- 📋 Cinnamon Pass tops out at 12,600 ft and Engineer Pass at 12,800 ft; the western switchbacks between Animas Forks and Engineer's summit are narrow, rocky and exposed. From Lake City, 2WD reaches Capitol City and American Basin *(source: lakecity.com "Driving the Alpine Loop", search snippet, 2026-10-01)*
- 📋 Jeep Wrangler Rubicon rentals in Ouray reach Yankee Boy Basin, Imogene Pass and the Alpine Loop from Main Street *(source: coloradowestutvandjeeps.com, search snippet, 2026-10-01)*. Price TBD
- 📋 Renters report doing the full loop in a rented 4x4 *(source: tripadvisor, reddit r/ColoradoOffroad, search snippets, 2026-10-01)*
- ⚠️ Animas Forks ghost town is on the route. How explorable vs. stabilized it is: not checked (principle 10)

### 2026-10-03 · Free camping and showers
- Context: Colin daydreaming a dispersed-first drive-out western loop, showering at places that sell one. Not a standing preference *(stated 2026-10-03; `me/camping.md` still says developed + showers)*
- 📋 South Mineral Creek Road (FR 585), off US 550 about 2 mi from Silverton: free dispersed pullouts start right after the turn and run to the Ice Lakes trailhead *(source: facebook San Juan group, reddit r/ColoradoOffroad, search snippets, 2026-10-03)*. The USFS says the road "is drivable by 2WD" *(source: fs.usda.gov South Mineral Campground, search snippet, 2026-10-03)*. 2WD usually means a truck, not a low sedan: check before relying on it
- 📋 South Mineral Campground itself is paid, first-come *(source: thedyrt.com, search snippet, 2026-10-03)*
- 📋 Shower: Ouray Hot Springs Pool sells a **shower-only pass, $4, 30-minute limit**; adult day admission $30 *(source: visitouray.com, search snippet, 2026-10-03)*
- ⚠️ Silverton showers: not checked (search rate-limited)
