# Pittsburgh + Ohiopyle

*The first city trip, as a test run*

## Why

The cheapest way to find out whether a city trip actually works for Colin (see City trips in `me/travel-style.md`): a hotel base, one planned walking route, a named dish, then a state park and a campsite. Pittsburgh sits between home and [Ohiopyle](ohiopyle.md), so the park doesn't add a detour.

The rough shape:
1. **Avon → McConnells Mill State Park → Pittsburgh.** Kildoo Trail, about 2 mi along Slippery Rock Creek gorge, starting at the covered bridge beside the historic gristmill. Then check in to the hotel.
2. **City day.** The South Side Slopes step routes (about 2,500 steps in one neighborhood) and the Carrie Blast Furnaces Industrial Tour, with the route built around one named dish.
3. **→ Ohiopyle.** The Youghiogheny gorge, Cucumber Falls, and the Meadow Run natural waterslides in summer. Camp at Kentuck.
4. **Home.**

**The timing tradeoff is undecided** *(stated 2026-09-29)*:
- **May–October** gets both the furnace tours and the slides, but in 2027 that means spending a weekend of the last free summer on something reachable any weekend.
- **A school-year Fri–Mon** costs nothing against the horizon, but has no tours and no slides.
- **After Aug 2027 on work vacation** is the case this trip type is made for.

## Next

Pick a timing. Then check the drive times, which are only recalled (~2 h Avon → Pittsburgh, ~1h15 Pittsburgh → Ohiopyle), and choose the one named dish the city day is built around.

## Research notes

Dated entries, newest last. What was found while daydreaming, with a source on every fact. `/daydream` writes here.

### 2026-09-29 · First pass
- ✅ Pittsburgh has more public staircases than any US city *(source: pittsburghpa.gov City Steps, 2026-09-29)*. Counts vary: 675–800+ *(source: search snippets, 2026-09-29)*.
- 📋 South Side Slopes: about 2,500 steps. StepTrek publishes self-paced route maps, and the historic routes are on the neighborhood site *(source: southsideslopes.org and r/pittsburgh, search snippets, 2026-09-29)*.
- ✅ Carrie Blast Furnaces Industrial Tour: morning and afternoon tours, **May through October**, limited to 20 people, "regularly sell out", buy ahead *(source: riversofsteel.com/experiences/tours/industrial-tour, 2026-09-29)*. Built 1907, operated until 1978 *(source: search snippet, 2026-09-29)*.
- ✅ Festival of Combustion at Carrie, Sat Oct 3 2026, 3–9 pm, $25 advance *(source: riversofsteel.com, 2026-09-29)*.
- ✅ McConnells Mill: Kildoo Trail starts at either end of the covered bridge, south of the gristmill; Gorge Trail is 6.2 mi, rated "most difficult" *(source: pa.gov DCNR hiking page, search snippet, 2026-09-29)*. 📋 Kildoo is about 2.0 mi as a loop *(source: hikesandhops.org, 2026-09-29)*.
- ✅ Ohiopyle (Kentuck) campground: hot showers, flush toilets, open through Dec 12 2026, tent sites bookable same day and up to 11 months ahead *(source: pennsylvaniastateparks.reserveamerica.com, 2026-09-29)*.
- 📋 Meadow Run slides run "during summer months when water levels and temperatures are appropriate" *(source: ohiopyle.org, search snippet, 2026-09-29)*.
- ⚠️ Not checked: drive times, a sunset spot (Mount Washington via the inclines is recalled, not verified), hotel area, parking, and the named dish.
