### 2026-10-10 · The chosen route: "Granite & Gold: Tunnel Chute, Two Ghost-Mine Ruins and a Night Soak"
- Picked by Colin from the showdown *(2026-10-10)*. This is the builder's final version after the fact-check (grade B). Labels as the builder wrote them: ✅ read on the page, 📋 snippet, ⚠️ estimate. **Every drive time marked ⚠️ is unrouted, and every "sunset roughly X" still needs computing.** August 2027 prices are TBD.
- **Fly**: Reno-Tahoe (RNO), Sat Aug 7 2027. There's no nonstop from CLE. Every routing has one stop, and the fastest is about 6h15 (Google Flights 📋). Pick a flight that lands by about 2pm so the Edwards Crossing evening swim works. Out Reno-Tahoe (RNO), Sun Aug 15 2027, on a flight leaving after about 2pm.
- **Car**: Enterprise RNO minivan (Pacifica class). Enterprise's RNO page (✅) lets 21–24s rent minivans but not 7-seat SUVs; $25/day underage fee; additional drivers $15/day each (national FAQ); whether each extra young driver also pays $25/day is TBD. The damage waiver is void for damage caused by unpaved roads, and the route has four short dirt stretches (Bodie's last 3 mi, Travertine 1.1, Wild Willy's ~1.5, Panum 0.9).
- **Bases**: Nevada City, CA ×2 (Outside Inn's two-bedroom cottage: two king beds plus a sofa bed, sleeps up to 6, from $240/night plus $15 per extra person over double occupancy (the room page says $15; the FAQ says $10, a conflict)); South Lake Tahoe, CA ×3 (Two motel rooms with 2 queens each, or one 2BR); June Lake, CA ×3 (Double Eagle Resort two-bedroom cabin: sleeps 6, kitchen, deck BBQ, $299-349/night (allcabins).

**Day 1 · Sat Aug 7 2027 · travel · Land in Reno, evening swim at Edwards Crossing**

Land at RNO and pick up the Enterprise minivan. Get groceries and small bills: Angora's and Vikingsholm's parking both take exact cash.
Drive RNO to Nevada City via I-80/CA-20, about 85 mi, roughly 1h45 with the buffer (⚠️ estimate). Check in to the Outside Inn.
About 5:30pm: drive North Bloomfield Rd to Edwards Crossing. Park only fully off the pavement and outside every posted no-parking zone. Nevada County tickets illegal parking here from $162 and tows; parking on the bridge is prohibited (Nevada County Sheriff 2024, KNCO 2025, via the fact-checker). If there's no legal spot, skip it.
- Walk upstream to the pools. SmartLeap describes them as about 20 ft deep with jumps of 10-20 ft (📋, single source). Check depth yourself and start on the low ledges.
- Jumping off the bridge is prohibited.
- Swim until the canyon goes gold. Sunset is roughly 8pm; compute it, don't trust this.
Dinner: Lola at the National Exchange Hotel. Their Saturday kitchen runs until 10pm (thenationalexchangehotel.com, via the fact-checker), so a late swim still works. The menu 'changes regularly'. In April 2025 it listed a Porterhouse Pork Chop at $37 (Yelp 📋). Order it if it's still on; the 2027 menu is TBD.

*Bail-out:* If you land late or tired, skip Edwards and walk downtown Nevada City before dinner.

**Day 2 · Sun Aug 8 2027 · big · Class IV on the Middle Fork American: Tunnel Chute, Kanaka Falls, 18 miles**

Leave Nevada City at 7:30. Auburn is about 25 mi down Hwy 49, roughly 40 min with the buffer (⚠️ estimate).
WET Middle Fork 1-Day:
- Meets behind Raley's, 13384 Lincoln Way, Auburn. The meet time is CONFLICTED: 8:30am on the trip page, but the 2-day page shows an 8:00 start. Confirm when booking.
- Then a van shuttle to the Oxbow Reservoir put-in. 18 miles to Ruck-a-Chucky, Class IV, minimum age 12, swimmers only, deli lunch. Runs about 8:30am-5pm (raftwet.com 📋).
- WET's price page lists the 1-day trip at $134 on Sundays, $129 Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri, $100 Wednesdays and $139-149 Saturdays (raftwet.com/trip-prices 📋; the page doesn't show its year). WET itself says midweek or Sunday is the best time to avoid Saturday crowds.
- Flow is regulated by Oxbow Dam for rafting May through September, even in dry years (raftwet.com 📋).
The rapids: Good Morning first. About 2.5 mi in comes the Tunnel Chute, a mining-era chute with a 6-ft drop into a 90-ft rock tunnel. Raft California calls it IV+ (raftcalifornia.com 📋). Then Kanaka Falls, a steep Class IV drop (whitewaterexcitement.com 📋). After lunch it's Class III-IV rapids like Cleavage, Parallel Parking and Texas Chainsaw. The canyon walls are 2,000 ft deep.
Other outfitters running the same stretch:
- Sierra Whitewater: $159 weekday / $169 weekend + $9 river fees; weekends by request only.
- H2O Adventures: $99-156, meets 8:15 in Auburn.
- Whitewater Excitement: $139-169.
Back at the Auburn lot around 5. Nevada City by about 6.
Dinner: Sherwood (the former Friar Tuck's, remodeled 2026). Sunday hours are TBD; the fact-checker found Fri-Sat 5-9pm and closed Wednesdays. Order the Onion Bread ($9, a 1973 holdover) for the table plus a cooked main. Colin skips the Sherwood Fondue, which comes with 'seasonal fruit & vegetables'. If Sherwood is closed on Sunday, go to Lola (Sun-Thu 5-9pm).

*Bail-out:* The guides make every river call. Anyone can walk Tunnel Chute if the guide offers it (⚠️ standard outfitter practice, confirm with WET). Ruck-a-Chucky Falls is Class VI and is only viewed from land (raft-h2o.com 📋). If anyone doesn't want Class IV, book the South Fork full-river run that day instead: Class III, WET from $144, ends 4:30 (raftwet.com ✅ fact-checker). Smoke: rafting still works at moderate AQI.

**Day 3 · Mon Aug 9 2027 · medium · Weekday morning at Hoyt's Crossing, over the mountains to Tahoe, sunset on the beach**

Be at the South Yuba River SP lot at the Hwy 49 crossing by 8:00. Going on a weekday dodges the weekend crowds and the CHP ticketing: 21 illegal-parking tickets were written there in summer 2026 (sfgate 2026-09-03). Budget $10/vehicle in cash: the park's 2026 page lists $10 vehicle day use, and the lot fee is conflicted. Loaner life jackets are at the crossing (parks.ca.gov ✅). If Nevada County's $2 River Shuttle runs in 2027 (2026 pilot; 2027 TBD), use it instead.
Hike Hoyt's Trail about 1.2 mi to the beach (sierragoldparksfoundation.org ✅). Swim the pools. The beach is clothing-optional.
The 'hidden jump rock' 20-45 min further up is UNVERIFIED. It comes from SmartLeap and one substack only: a cable section down slick granite, takeoffs reported at about 3-16 ft. Go only if the group wants the scramble. Inspect the landing and depth in person, and jump only where someone has swum the landing first.
Back at the car by about 12:30. Lunch in Nevada City.
Drive to South Lake Tahoe via I-80, Truckee and CA-89, about 2h30 with the buffer (⚠️ estimate). Check in.
Sunset: The Beacon Bar & Grill at Camp Richardson, on the sand. Order the Famous Fish & Chips and one Rum Runner each (spirits, no beer; menu 📋, not re-verified).

*Bail-out:* Turnaround: Hoyt's beach. If the lot is full or anyone isn't feeling the river, swap in Malakoff Diggins (about 40 min from Nevada City):
- Walk the 2.6-mi Diggins Loop across the floor of the hydraulic-mining pit and the 1850s town of North Bloomfield, self-guided. $10/vehicle, paid at the museum, open 10am-6pm in summer. Tour days conflict (daily vs Saturdays only), so call ahead (parks.ca.gov ✅ fact-checker).
- North Bloomfield Rd has unpaved sections; see the CDW note.
The river warning: Hoyt's is 'more treacherous than other areas of the park' (southyubariverstatepark.org ✅). Diving is prohibited.

**Day 4 · Tue Aug 10 2027 · medium · Upper Angora jumps, the waterfalls and 1863 resort at Glen Alpine**

Be at the Angora Lakes lot by 9:00. Parking is $12, exact cash, and the 'parking area may be full'. The season runs mid-June to mid-September (fs.usda.gov, updated 2026-08-31 ✅).
Walk 0.7 mi up to Upper Angora Lake: 1.4 mi out and back, 246 ft gain (AllTrails ✅). Granite ledges rise over a small resort beach. Ledge heights are reported at 10-65 ft (Tahoe Daily Tribune 2021) and 16/30/40 ft (Reddit) (📋).
Group ceiling: about 16 ft, 25 ft hard max, feet first.
Rent a rowboat or kayak: $26/hr in 2023, card required, back by 5pm (wandertahoe.com 📋; 2027 TBD). The resort's fresh-squeezed lemonade is raw lemon, so Colin decides against his raw-fruit rule.
Early afternoon: drive the narrow one-lane Fallen Leaf Rd to the Glen Alpine trailhead near Lily Lake. Walk about 1 mi each way past Glen Alpine Falls and Modjeska Falls to Glen Alpine Springs. It was founded around 1863 (Wikipedia 📋), and six of its buildings are by Bernard Maybeck (glenalpinesprings.com ✅ fact-checker). Hard reality: the buildings open only 'when volunteers are on site... you might catch us there on the weekends' (glenalpinesprings.com/faq). On a Tuesday, plan to see the grounds and exteriors only. The waterfalls are the real draw. Swim at Lily Lake or Fallen Leaf Lake if it's hot.
Dinner: casual in South Lake Tahoe (venue TBD), early night.

*Bail-out:* If Angora's lot is full, flip the order: Glen Alpine first, then Angora late afternoon (rentals close at 5). Hard rules at Angora:
- Nothing above 25 ft.
- No flips.
- Nobody jumps alone.
- One spotter in the water.
Two men died here: one from about 50 ft in 1996, one attempting a flip in 1993 (Tahoe Daily Tribune 2001 📋). There is no current official statement on whether jumping is allowed. The 2001 Forest Service quote calling it legal is 25 years old, so obey any posted signs.

**Day 5 · Wed Aug 11 2027 · medium · Emerald Bay: kayak to Tahoe's only island and the stone tea-house shell, then the Rubicon cliffs**

Be at the Vikingsholm/Emerald Bay lot on Hwy 89 by about 9:15. The pay machine is $10 and takes exact change only (recent review 📋), and the lot fills fast.
Walk the 1 mi, 500-ft paved descent to Vikingsholm (parks.ca.gov 📋).
Kayak FIRST, because parks.ca.gov says 'mornings are best' and afternoon wind regularly shuts paddling down.
- Kayak Tahoe's stand opens at 10, first come first served.
- Double kayak $55/1hr or $85/2hr on an older page; a recent review paid $80 for 2 hrs (2027 TBD). Bring cash: 'Credit Card transactions often times can not be run'. Paddlers must be over 15.
- Paddle to Fannette Island, Lake Tahoe's only island, and climb to the tea house, where 'only the stone shell remains' (parks.ca.gov ✅ fact-checker). The nesting closure dates conflict between sources, but every version ends before August.
- Swimming to the island is prohibited.
After the paddle, walk the Rubicon Trail north along the cliff-top shore as an out-and-back, 2-3 hrs (the trail is 7.4 mi end to end; parks.ca.gov ✅).
NO jumping anywhere along the Rubicon or D.L. Bliss: the park lists 'No Jumping/Diving' (parks.ca.gov ✅). Climb back to the car. Optional: Lower Eagle Falls.
Evening: lakeshore swim and a motel night off.

*Bail-out:* If the wind is up or the stand is closed, skip the paddle and walk the Rubicon further. Last rentals leave at 4pm, and you must be in line by 3:45 (kayaktahoe.com ✅). In the 7-night version, this is the day you cut.

**Day 6 · Thu Aug 12 2027 · travel · Over to the desert: Travertine soak, Bodie ghost town, Mono Lake float at sunset**

Leave South Lake Tahoe by 8:30 for Bridgeport via US-395, about 2h15 (⚠️ estimate).
Travertine Hot Springs: BLM, free. Take Jack Sawyer Rd just south of Bridgeport, then about 1.1 mi of dirt, fine for a sedan when dry (catraveltimes.com 2026 ✅ fact-checker). The source runs near 180F, so test the water and soak only in the lower pools. Clothing-optional. BLM closes it with locked gates around the solstices and equinoxes (Jun 17-23 and Sep 19-25 in 2025); Aug 12 should be clear, but check the 2027 order.
Lunch in Bridgeport.
Bodie SHP: $8 per adult, 9am-6pm in summer, 30-40 min from US-395 with the last 3 mi on dirt (parks.ca.gov ✅). Arrive by 1:45 for about 3.5 hrs in the arrested-decay town. Anyone there after hours is cited.
Leave Bodie by 5:15. Bodie to Lee Vining is about 1h (⚠️ estimate).
EARLY DINNER at Whoa Nellie Deli in the Lee Vining Mobil at about 6:15. That fixes the closing conflict: the site says 9pm, the online ordering page says 8pm. Order the World Famous Fish Tacos with the wasabi crème fraîche ON THE SIDE because of the spice ceiling, or the wild buffalo meatloaf (whoanelliedeli.com 📋).
Then 10-15 min to South Tufa (⚠️ estimate) by about 7:15 and float. The water is denser than the ocean, so you float easily, but it's alkaline: keep it out of your eyes and any cuts. Expect a mucky shore with brine flies (⚠️). Bring a jug of fresh water to rinse off.
Stay among the tufa towers for sunset, around 8pm; compute it. The County Park boardwalk burned in the 2025 Inn Fire; South Tufa is the access either way.
About 20 min to June Lake.

*Bail-out:* If you're running late, skip Travertine: Bodie's 6pm close is the hard deadline. Check AQI before leaving Tahoe (see risks).

**Day 7 · Fri Aug 13 2027 · big · The big one: Clouds Rest, looking down on Half Dome, then a hot-spring soak**

Leave June Lake at 5:45. Pay Yosemite's $35/vehicle entrance at Tioga Pass. It's card only and good for 7 days, so it also covers tomorrow (yosemite.org 2026 ✅ fact-checker; 2027 fee TBD).
Drive to the trailhead at the west end of Tenaya Lake, about 1h (⚠️ estimate). The lot is small.
Clouds Rest Trail: 12.6 mi out and back, 3,038 ft gain, Hard, 4.9 stars (AllTrails ✅). The summit is 9,930 ft, roughly 1,000 ft above Half Dome, which you look down on. No permit is needed to day-hike. The final stretch is a narrow, exposed granite ridge. Be off the summit by 1pm.
Back by about 3pm. Swim in Tenaya Lake, then stop at Olmsted Point.
Around 6:30, cabin BBQ on the Double Eagle deck.
At dusk: Wild Willy's (Crowley) Hot Spring as recovery for sore legs. Take 395 south to Benton Crossing Rd, then about 1.5 mi of dirt and a flat 0.3-mi boardwalk. Free BLM pools at 98-102F, clothing-optional, slick mud edges (swimholesmap.com ✅ fact-checker). About 35 min each way (⚠️ estimate). Friday beats Saturday: the lot 'fills on summer weekends', and camping is allowed in the lot.
The driver is whoever is least wrecked. Watch for deer and open-range cattle on 395 at night (⚠️).

*Bail-out:* Turnaround: 12:00 at the base of the summit ridge, whoever is in front. Build-ups or thunder mean turn around immediately. The ridge is optional; anyone uneasy waits at its foot.
Altitude: the hike runs 8,150-9,930 ft. Headache, nausea or confusion means everyone goes down together.
If legs or eyelids are done, skip Wild Willy's tonight. Skip it in rain either way: the spur wants high clearance when wet, and the CDW doesn't cover the dirt.

**Day 8 · Sat Aug 14 2027 · medium · 1870s mine ruins at 10,800 ft, then into a volcano at golden hour**

Slow start. About 9:30, drive back up to Tioga Pass; yesterday's pass still covers you.
Great Sierra Mine via Gaylor Lakes: 3.9 mi out and back, 1,256 ft gain, max 10,797 ft, Hard, about 2.5 hrs (AllTrails ✅). It starts at the Tioga Pass Entrance lot. Climb to a ridge over Dana Meadows, drop to Middle Gaylor Lake, then go up past Upper Gaylor to the stone ruins of the abandoned 1870s mine.
Lunch at Whoa Nellie Deli. Afternoon: nap, June Lake beach.
Golden hour: Panum Crater. Take CA-120 3.1 mi east from 395, then 0.9 mi of dirt (USGS 📋). A 2.1-mi trail circles the rim of a plug-dome volcano that erupted about 700 years ago, with paths into the crater and up the obsidian-and-pumice dome, and Mono Lake below (Mammoth Lakes Rec 📋).
Last-night dinner at the cabin.

*Bail-out:* If legs are shot after Clouds Rest, skip Gaylor. Take the Devils Postpile/Rainbow Falls shuttle from Mammoth instead ($15 📋, not re-verified; the Saturday 8am line can be long), or just do the lake beach.
Altitude: Gaylor tops out near 10,800 ft the day after Clouds Rest. Anyone who had symptoms yesterday sits this one out.
If you don't want the Panum dirt road, watch sunset from South Tufa again (paved).
