# Sipsey Wilderness

*Land of a Thousand Waterfalls*

## Why

⚠️ The largest designated wilderness east of the Mississippi, with dozens of waterfalls, sandstone box canyons, huge old growth and swimming holes. **The one place on the whole list whose peak window is early spring specifically** — its waterfalls only run with the water. Day-hike into the wilderness from a drive-up base rather than backpacking it.

✅ **Corrected Sept 2026 — the earlier "open April–October" note here was wrong.** **Clear Creek opens the second Friday of March** and closes Oct 31; reservation-only via recreation.gov. **Corinth ran Mar 8–Oct 31 in 2026**, part reservable and part first-come. So the season starts in early-to-mid March, not April — but a trip starting in the first week of March has **no open established campground in Bankhead**.
