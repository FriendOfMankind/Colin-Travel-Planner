### 2026-10-29 · Flights, the city choice, Iceland
- 📋 **Dublin–Athens nonstop:** Aegean, Aer Lingus and Ryanair, about 10 flights a week, ~4h15 *(source: flightsfrom.com/DUB-ATH)*. One-ways average about $134 *(source: Kayak, 2026-10-22)*.
- 📋 **Dublin–Marseille:** Ryanair, plus Aer Lingus from 2027-03-30, 3 flights a week, ~2h35, about $170 round trip *(source: flightsfrom.com/DUB-MRS)*.
- 📋 **Kerry Airport flies to:** Dublin, London, Manchester, Frankfurt-Hahn, Faro and Alicante. No Athens or Marseille *(source: kerryairport.ie)*. Kerry–Dublin is 55 min on Ryanair *(source: ryanair.com)*.
- 📋 **Aer Lingus Cleveland–Dublin,** May 5–12, 2027: from $700.89 *(source: aerlingus.com, 2026-09-30)*.
- 📋 **US customs is cleared in Dublin** before boarding US-bound flights *(source: dublinairport.com)*.
- 📋 **Athens in May:** about 25°C with ~14 h of light. Orthodox Easter is May 2, 2027. Cruise ships are in port 27 of 31 days *(source: avoid-crowds.com, travelcharika.com, 2026-10)*.
- 📋 **Calanques:** April–May is the best hiking of the year *(source: marseille-spirit.com, 2026-07)*. The park is open Oct 1–May 31, with fire rules from June 1 *(source: marseille-tourisme.com)*.
- Athens and Marseille costs, neighborhoods and day plans were researched for a March version on 2026-10-27/28. That chat wasn't saved, so redo it if needed.

Starting it later only eats Acadia or the Gros Morne ferry buffer. The turnaround gets fixed on this trip's side *(Claude's reasoning, 2026-10-29)*.
- 📋 **EI86 Cleveland → Dublin, summer 2026:** Mon–Wed and Fri–Sun, 20:10 → 08:10 next day, A321neo *(source: info.flightmapper.net, 2026-10-29)*. 📋 Six a week, no Thursdays *(source: aerospaceglobalnews.com, 2026-05; travelextra.ie, 2025-10)*. 📋 JobsOhio's revenue guarantee runs through 2027 *(source: travelextra.ie, 2025-10)*.
- 📋 **Kerry → Dublin (FR612):** morning departures ran 09:25–09:50 in Sep 2026, landing ~10:20–10:45. There's also a ~17:35 evening flight. Dublin → Kerry ran 09:05 and ~16:35 *(source: flightradar24 FR612 and FR5307, trip.com, momondo.ie, 2026-10-29)*.
- 📋 **Dublin → Athens days (winter timetable):** Mon Aegean + Ryanair, Tue–Wed Ryanair, Thu Aer Lingus + Ryanair, Fri Aegean, Sun Aer Lingus. Ryanair left ~18:20 *(source: kayak.com, ryanair.com, 2026-10-29)*. ⚠️ The summer 2027 timetable may differ.
- 📋 **Athens → US nonstops:** United to Newark (UA125, 10:25 → 14:05 daily, Mar 6 – Nov 19, 2026), Washington Dulles and Chicago. Delta to JFK, Atlanta and Boston. American to JFK (from 2027-05-28), Chicago (from 2027-05-07), Philadelphia, Charlotte and Dallas *(source: greekairports.gr United press release 2026-05-29; flightsfrom.com ATH-JFK and ATH-ORD; pappaspost.com 2026-09-22)*. ⚠️ Full 2027 dates aren't published yet.
- 📋 **Delphi:** 180 km from Athens. 2.5–3 h by car, ~3 h by KTEL bus from Liosion station (3–4 a day). A day trip runs 10–12 h door to door, and every source recommends staying overnight in Delphi or Arachova to be in ahead of the coaches *(source: angelsathens.gr 2026-03, athenstourism.org 2026-10, mygreecetours.org 2026-06)*.
- 📋 **Meteora:** ~341–350 km. A day trip is possible by train to Kalambaka or by tour, but an overnight is "preferable" and catches the sunset *(source: athenslover.com, visitmeteora.travel, mygreecetours.org 2026-06)*.
- 📋 **Nafplio + Mycenae + Epidaurus:** called the best full-day trip for ruins plus a pleasant town. Tours from ~€65 *(source: daytripsfromathens.com, 2026-02)*.
- 📋 **Aegina:** 40 min by hydrofoil or 1h15 by ferry from Piraeus, frequent, no need to book *(source: lonelyplanet.com)*. **Sounion:** half-day trip, ~2 h by bus *(same)*.
- ⚠️ A US licence may need an International Driving Permit to rent in Greece *(recalled, not checked)*. Check before choosing the car.
