# Kerry + Athens (May, with a friend)

*Four nights with a friend's family in Kerry, plus one warm, ancient city, squeezed between Commencement and Newfoundland*

## Start here (handoff for a new chat)

**What's settled** *(stated 2026-10-29)*:
- A friend is committed to Europe in May. **The transatlantic flight is paid** (by his family).
- He's visiting family in **County Kerry, not Dingle** (was: "his grandparents in Dingle", stated 2026-10-12). **Four nights there.** Colin joins.
- **The friend comes to the city leg too.**
- Colin has to be home in time for Newfoundland (leaves Sun May 23).
- **Iceland on the way home: no, for this trip** (reasons in the notes below).
- **Dublin: skip it,** or one night only if a flight time forces it.

**Still open (ask these first):**
1. **Athens or Marseille.** My pick is Athens (tradeoffs below). Colin hasn't chosen.
2. **Is the transatlantic flight booked yet?** If not, book it as a multi-city ticket: **Cleveland → Athens on Aer Lingus via Dublin** on the way out. That way a late connection is the airline's problem. On separate tickets, it's Colin's. Aer Lingus won't change the origin of a booked return *(source: aerlingus.com, read 2026-10-12)*.
3. **Where in Kerry?** The town decides whether Kerry Airport (Farranfore), Cork or a drive is the way in and out.
4. **Exact flight dates.** Oct 12 looked at EI86 out Mon May 10 / EI87 back Tue May 18, ~$701. That came from decoding a link, so it's unconfirmed. Leaving Sun May 9 buys a day.
5. **Home turnaround.** Coming back ~May 20 leaves only 2 days to repack for Newfoundland. Decide whether that's enough.

## Why

Free beds and a paid flight make the value. The city half adds what Kerry doesn't have: heat, ancient ruins and a real city to walk *(Claude's reasoning, 2026-10-29)*. Kerry already covers sea cliffs, coastal hiking and stone ruins, so a second coast adds less than a contrast does.

## The route (draft, ⚠️ every day depends on airline schedules)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| ~May 10 | Cleveland → Dublin overnight, Aer Lingus nonstop |
| ~May 11 | Land in Dublin, connect to Athens the same day (~4h15). Arrive evening |
| May 11–15 | **Athens, 4 nights** |
| ~May 15 | Athens → Dublin, then Dublin → Kerry (Ryanair, 55 min) the same day. A Dublin night only if the arrival is too late |
| May 15–19 | **Kerry, 4 nights** with family |
| ~May 19–20 | Kerry → Dublin by the morning flight, then the afternoon Aer Lingus to Cleveland. US customs is cleared in Dublin |
| May 20–22 | Home: repack for Newfoundland |

**City first, Kerry second, because:**
- Skellig Michael landing odds improve later in May.
- It ends next to the flight home.
- Jet lag lands in the city, not on the family.

**⚠️ The risky leg is Kerry → Dublin → Cleveland on separate tickets** the same day. The 2026 EI87 left Dublin at 15:40–16:10 *(source: flightstats, 2026-10-12)*. A morning Kerry flight leaves a big buffer. If it doesn't run that day, that's the one "convenient" Dublin night.

## Athens vs. Marseille, for this trip

| | Athens | Marseille |
|---|---|---|
| Contrast with Kerry | High: warm, ancient, a real city | Lower: more sea cliffs and coast |
| Hiking | Gentle (Hymettus, Parnitha) | **The best.** The Calanques' peak season is Apr–May, and the park is fully open until May 31 |
| City, ruins, nights out with a friend | **Better** | Good, grittier |
| Flights from Dublin | **~10 a week**, ~4h15, about $134 one way | **3 a week**, ~2h35, ~$170 round trip. The dates bend to the schedule |
| Costs on the ground | **Cheaper** | Higher (France) |
| Crowds | Cruise ships in port 27 of 31 May days, so do sights early | Calmer |
| Risk | Level 1, short-notice strikes | Level 2, strikes |

**Pick Athens** unless hiking is the main point, in which case Marseille.

## Research notes

### 2026-10-12 · The Dingle version (from an earlier chat, saved here 2026-10-29)
- The plan was 6 nights in Dingle with free beds, then 1 in Dublin. **Superseded:** now 4 nights, somewhere in Kerry.
- Kerry ideas, all *recalled* and unchecked:
  - Slea Head beehive huts, Dunbeg fort, Gallarus Oratory
  - The abandoned village on Great Blasket (the ferry runs in summer only; mid-May is unconfirmed)
  - Mount Brandon, Killarney and the Gap of Dunloe
  - Tom Crean's South Pole Inn in Annascaul
- Skellig Michael landings: the 2026 season started May 9, capped at 180 people a day, booking out well ahead. Bad seas allowed landings on only 3 days in the first week *(source: skelligislands.com, Irish Times 2026-05-17, read 2026-10-12)*. **This is the one booking that needs doing early.** Keep a backup day.
- Kerry to Dublin is about 4.5–5 h by road *(recalled, not routed)*.

### 2026-10-29 · Flights, the city choice, Iceland
- 📋 **Dublin–Athens nonstop:** Aegean, Aer Lingus and Ryanair, about 10 flights a week, ~4h15 *(source: flightsfrom.com/DUB-ATH)*. One-ways average about $134 *(source: Kayak, 2026-10-22)*.
- 📋 **Dublin–Marseille:** Ryanair, plus Aer Lingus from 2027-03-30, 3 flights a week, ~2h35, about $170 round trip *(source: flightsfrom.com/DUB-MRS)*.
- 📋 **Kerry Airport flies to:** Dublin, London, Manchester, Frankfurt-Hahn, Faro and Alicante. No Athens or Marseille *(source: kerryairport.ie)*. Kerry–Dublin is 55 min on Ryanair *(source: ryanair.com)*.
- 📋 **Aer Lingus Cleveland–Dublin,** May 5–12, 2027: from $700.89 *(source: aerlingus.com, 2026-09-30)*.
- 📋 **US customs is cleared in Dublin** before boarding US-bound flights *(source: dublinairport.com)*.
- 📋 **Athens in May:** about 25°C with ~14 h of light. Orthodox Easter is May 2, 2027. Cruise ships are in port 27 of 31 days *(source: avoid-crowds.com, travelcharika.com, 2026-10)*.
- 📋 **Calanques:** April–May is the best hiking of the year *(source: marseille-spirit.com, 2026-07)*. The park is open Oct 1–May 31, with fire rules from June 1 *(source: marseille-tourisme.com)*.
- Athens and Marseille costs, neighborhoods and day plans were researched for a March version on 2026-10-27/28. That chat wasn't saved, so redo it if needed.
- **Iceland on the way home: declined for May** *(Claude's call, 2026-10-29; Colin hasn't contested it)*:
  - 📋 Reykjavík's average May low is 4.0°C (39°F) *(source: iceland.org, WMO normals)*. Nights run 0–4°C and a −5°C comfort bag is recommended *(source: iceland-camping-equipment.com, 2026-04)*. Colin is comfortable down to the 40s °F, and 32°F is untested (`me/camping.md`, `me/gear.md`).
  - 📋 The official campsite season starts June 1, and highland roads are closed in May *(same source)*.
  - It also costs Newfoundland prep days, and it would mean carrying camping gear through two cities.
- **Keep for the real Iceland trip:** 📋 Icelandair includes a free stopover of up to 7 days on transatlantic tickets, and flies Dublin–Pittsburgh *(source: icelandair.com)*. Pittsburgh is ~2 h from home ⚠️. PLAY ended its US flights *(source: businesstraveller.com, 2025-06)*.
