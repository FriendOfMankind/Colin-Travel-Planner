# Athens + Kerry (May, with a friend)

*Two nights with a friend's family in Kerry, then a week of Athens and the mainland beyond it, squeezed between Commencement and Newfoundland*

## Start here (handoff for a new chat)

**What's settled:**
- A friend is committed to Europe in May. **A transatlantic flight is paid** by his family *(stated 2026-10-29)*. Whose seat that covers isn't recorded (see open question 1).
- He's out of school **May 1**, so he does his Kerry family visit first and Colin joins at the end of it *(stated 2026-10-29)*.
- **Kerry: 2 nights** (was: 4) *(stated 2026-10-29: "honestly worth the variety")*. **Then the friend comes to Greece with Colin.**
- **The city is Athens, and the point is to max it out** (was: Athens or Marseille, open) *(stated 2026-10-29: "I want to athensmaxx")*. That means **Athens as a base plus day trips and 1–2 overnights** for whatever is a must-do *(stated 2026-10-29)*.
- **Home by Thu May 20** *(stated 2026-10-29)*. That leaves May 21–22 for Newfoundland, which leaves Sun May 23.
- **Dublin: skip it.** Iceland on the way home: declined for May (reasons in the notes below).

**Still open (ask these first):**
1. **Whose seat does the family's money cover?** If it's Colin's, the ticket is probably a Cleveland ↔ Dublin round trip, and that breaks the "fly home from Athens" plan below. If it's only the friend's, Colin books his own open-jaw: Cleveland → Dublin out, Athens → Cleveland home.
2. **Where in Kerry?** The town decides whether Kerry Airport (Farranfore) works for the Dublin connection.
3. **Delphi + Meteora as a 2-night car loop, or Delphi alone with 1 night?** My pick is the loop (reasons under "Greece").
4. **Car or bus/train** for the overnights.
5. **Turnaround:** home the evening of May 20, out May 23. Load the Sienna for Newfoundland *before* finals week, so those 2 days are laundry and groceries only.

## Why

The friend and a free bed make Kerry cheap, and 2 nights there is enough variety. The money and the days go to Greece. What Greece adds is heat, the oldest ruins Colin can reach, and a real city to walk *(Claude's reasoning, 2026-10-29)*. **Meteora is the most Colin-shaped thing in reach:** sandstone towers that raise "why does this look like this?", with trails between the monasteries and a sunset viewpoint (`me/travel-style.md`) *(Claude's reasoning, 2026-10-29)*.

## Research notes

### 2026-10-12 · The Dingle version (from an earlier chat, saved here 2026-10-29)
- The plan was 6 nights in Dingle with free beds, then 1 in Dublin. **Superseded:** now 2 nights, somewhere in Kerry.
- Kerry ideas, all *recalled* and unchecked:
  - Slea Head beehive huts, Dunbeg fort, Gallarus Oratory
  - The abandoned village on Great Blasket (the ferry runs in summer only; mid-May is unconfirmed)
  - Mount Brandon, Killarney and the Gap of Dunloe
  - Tom Crean's South Pole Inn in Annascaul
- Skellig Michael landings: the 2026 season started May 9, capped at 180 people a day, booking out well ahead. Bad seas allowed landings on only 3 days in the first week *(source: skelligislands.com, Irish Times 2026-05-17, read 2026-10-12)*. **With 2 nights on May 10–12, it's a long shot:** the first days of the season, and no backup day. Don't plan around it.
- Kerry to Dublin is about 4.5–5 h by road *(recalled, not routed)*.

### 2026-10-29 · Flights, the city choice, Iceland
- 📋 **Dublin–Athens nonstop:** Aegean, Aer Lingus and Ryanair, about 10 flights a week, ~4h15 *(source: flightsfrom.com/DUB-ATH)*. One-ways average about $134 *(source: Kayak, 2026-10-22)*.
- 📋 **Dublin–Marseille:** Ryanair, plus Aer Lingus from 2027-03-30, 3 flights a week, ~2h35, about $170 round trip *(source: flightsfrom.com/DUB-MRS)*.
- 📋 **Kerry Airport flies to:** Dublin, London, Manchester, Frankfurt-Hahn, Faro and Alicante. No Athens or Marseille *(source: kerryairport.ie)*. Kerry–Dublin is 55 min on Ryanair *(source: ryanair.com)*.
- 📋 **Aer Lingus Cleveland–Dublin,** May 5–12, 2027: from $700.89 *(source: aerlingus.com, 2026-09-30)*.
- 📋 **US customs is cleared in Dublin** before boarding US-bound flights *(source: dublinairport.com)*.
- 📋 **Athens in May:** about 25°C with ~14 h of light. Orthodox Easter is May 2, 2027. Cruise ships are in port 27 of 31 days *(source: avoid-crowds.com, travelcharika.com, 2026-10)*.
- 📋 **Calanques:** April–May is the best hiking of the year *(source: marseille-spirit.com, 2026-07)*. The park is open Oct 1–May 31, with fire rules from June 1 *(source: marseille-tourisme.com)*.
- Athens and Marseille costs, neighborhoods and day plans were researched for a March version on 2026-10-27/28. That chat wasn't saved, so redo it if needed.
- **Iceland on the way home: declined for May** *(Claude's call, 2026-10-29; Colin hasn't contested it)*:
  - 📋 Reykjavík's average May low is 4.0°C (39°F) *(source: iceland.org, WMO normals)*. Nights run 0–4°C and a −5°C comfort bag is recommended *(source: iceland-camping-equipment.com, 2026-04)*. Colin is comfortable down to the 40s °F, and 32°F is untested (`me/camping.md`, `me/gear.md`).
  - 📋 The official campsite season starts June 1, and highland roads are closed in May *(same source)*.
  - It also costs Newfoundland prep days, and it would mean carrying camping gear through two cities.
- **Keep for the real Iceland trip:** 📋 Icelandair includes a free stopover of up to 7 days on transatlantic tickets, and flies Dublin–Pittsburgh *(source: icelandair.com)*. Pittsburgh is ~2 h from home ⚠️. PLAY ended its US flights *(source: businesstraveller.com, 2025-06)*.

### 2026-10-29 · Athensmaxx: the reshape, and getting home
- **Decided by Colin:** 2 nights in Kerry, Athens as a base plus day trips and 1–2 overnights, home May 20 *(stated 2026-10-29)*. The friend is out of school May 1 *(stated 2026-10-29)*.
- **Marseille: dropped.** Colin wants Athens maxed out. The Calanques hiking was its only real edge *(stated 2026-10-29)*.
- **Why not "Newfoundland later" instead:** Newfoundland is pinned at both ends: the friends' week (Jun 10–19), the Argentia ferry from Jun 16, and ParksNL's Viking Trail sites opening ~May 29 *(see that trip's file)*. Starting it later only eats Acadia or the Gros Morne ferry buffer. The turnaround gets fixed on this trip's side *(Claude's reasoning, 2026-10-29)*.
- 📋 **EI86 Cleveland → Dublin, summer 2026:** Mon–Wed and Fri–Sun, 20:10 → 08:10 next day, A321neo *(source: info.flightmapper.net, 2026-10-29)*. 📋 Six a week, no Thursdays *(source: aerospaceglobalnews.com, 2026-05; travelextra.ie, 2025-10)*. 📋 JobsOhio's revenue guarantee runs through 2027 *(source: travelextra.ie, 2025-10)*.
- 📋 **Kerry → Dublin (FR612):** morning departures ran 09:25–09:50 in Sep 2026, landing ~10:20–10:45. There's also a ~17:35 evening flight. Dublin → Kerry ran 09:05 and ~16:35 *(source: flightradar24 FR612 and FR5307, trip.com, momondo.ie, 2026-10-29)*.
- 📋 **Dublin → Athens days (winter timetable):** Mon Aegean + Ryanair, Tue–Wed Ryanair, Thu Aer Lingus + Ryanair, Fri Aegean, Sun Aer Lingus. Ryanair left ~18:20 *(source: kayak.com, ryanair.com, 2026-10-29)*. ⚠️ The summer 2027 timetable may differ.
- 📋 **Athens → US nonstops:** United to Newark (UA125, 10:25 → 14:05 daily, Mar 6 – Nov 19, 2026), Washington Dulles and Chicago. Delta to JFK, Atlanta and Boston. American to JFK (from 2027-05-28), Chicago (from 2027-05-07), Philadelphia, Charlotte and Dallas *(source: greekairports.gr United press release 2026-05-29; flightsfrom.com ATH-JFK and ATH-ORD; pappaspost.com 2026-09-22)*. ⚠️ Full 2027 dates aren't published yet.
- 📋 **Delphi:** 180 km from Athens. 2.5–3 h by car, ~3 h by KTEL bus from Liosion station (3–4 a day). A day trip runs 10–12 h door to door, and every source recommends staying overnight in Delphi or Arachova to be in ahead of the coaches *(source: angelsathens.gr 2026-03, athenstourism.org 2026-10, mygreecetours.org 2026-06)*.
- 📋 **Meteora:** ~341–350 km. A day trip is possible by train to Kalambaka or by tour, but an overnight is "preferable" and catches the sunset *(source: athenslover.com, visitmeteora.travel, mygreecetours.org 2026-06)*.
- 📋 **Nafplio + Mycenae + Epidaurus:** called the best full-day trip for ruins plus a pleasant town. Tours from ~€65 *(source: daytripsfromathens.com, 2026-02)*.
- 📋 **Aegina:** 40 min by hydrofoil or 1h15 by ferry from Piraeus, frequent, no need to book *(source: lonelyplanet.com)*. **Sounion:** half-day trip, ~2 h by bus *(same)*.
- ⚠️ A US licence may need an International Driving Permit to rent in Greece *(recalled, not checked)*. Check before choosing the car.
