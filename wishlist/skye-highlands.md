## Research notes

### 2026-11-01 · First look
- ✅ **Glenbrittle Campsite & Cafe**: 160 pitches spread down to Loch Brittle beach below the Cuillin; shower block, laundry, café; **open 26 March – 15 October**; non-electric pitches are allocated on arrival, no booking *(source: dunvegancastle.com/glenbrittle, 2026-11-01)*
- 📋 **Rubh an Dùnain** from the campsite: 12.8 km there and back, ~4 h with exploring: an Iron Age fort, a Viking canal, a cairn and the abandoned MacAskill croft houses. River crossings can be tricky in high water *(source: isleofskye.com)*
- 📋 Coire Lagan, a lochan in the Cuillin, starts beside the campsite toilet block; Suisnish clearance village is ~10 mi away *(source: britainexpress.com, 2021-11)*
- 📋 **Midges:** late May to September; the worst stretch is the last week of June and the first three weeks of July; effectively over by mid-September. Wind above ~6 mph grounds them *(source: outdoorscot.co.uk, 2026-04; lovefromscotland.co.uk)*. Skye is among the worst places *(source: campfind.co.uk)*
- 📋 Scotland's right to roam covers tent camping on Skye *(source: campfind.co.uk)*

### 2026-11-10 · The Skye half of the May 2027 trip, as planned before it was cut
The May 9–22 trip (`trips/dingle-marseille-2027/`) carried a Scotland half from 2026-11-02 until 2026-11-10, when Colin, Finn and Stick picked Ireland only. What it had found, so a Skye trip starts ahead. Prices are 2026 observations; full sources and the r/Skye sweep are in that trip's `research-log.md` (2026-11-02 to 2026-11-04).
- **The shape:** fly Dublin → Inverness, a one-way car Inverness → Edinburgh Airport, Loch Ness and Eilean Donan to Skye, **3 nights in Portree** (Dunvegan day, Trotternish day, Fairy Pools), 1 in Glencoe for the Lost Valley.
- **Portree Youth Hostel**, Hostelling Scotland: 3 beds in a shared room, 3 nights, **£319.50**, paid in full at booking; nothing on Airbnb or Booking.com beat ~£330 *(Colin's screenshot, 2026-11-02)*. Skye beds are scarce: "For May to September, book months ahead" 📋 *(scotlandswild.com)*.
- ✅ **Dunvegan Castle**, the MacLeod chiefs' seat for about 800 years: open Apr 1 – Oct 15, 10:00–17:30, last entry 17:00 *(dunvegancastle.com)*; £17.50 📋 *(castlescotland.app)*. **Visiting MacLeods sign the clan's own visitors' book** ✅ *(clanmacleod.org)*.
- ✅ **Parking, 2026:** the Storr £6 for up to 6 hours, the Quiraing £6, Kilt Rock £3, the Fairy Pools £8 *(hiddenscotland.com; Highland Council parking review, 2026-02)*.
- ✅ **The Lost Valley (Coire Gabhail):** 3.1 mi out and back, 980 ft, stepping stones over the river *(AllTrails, 2026-11-02)*.
- **Flights from Dublin:** Aer Lingus Dublin → Inverness 13:45 → 15:20, $358 for 3; Edinburgh → Dublin 15:30, $296 for 3 *(Colin's screenshots, 2026-11-04)*.
- **Cars for a 22-year-old:** Europcar UK rents from 22, about £30 a day per under-25 driver 📋 *(myhighlands.de)*; Enterprise and Alamo at Glasgow want 25 *(their branch pages)*. Kayak, May 17–22: automatic $392–404 for 5 days *(2026-11-02)*.
- **On the ground in May:** midges come out some time in May and are rarely bad that early; quiet before 9 AM, busy from ~10 📋 *(independenttravelcats.com)*; Fairy Pools "a theme park with the crowds", locals point to Glen Brittle instead *(r/Skye, 2026-06-23)*.
