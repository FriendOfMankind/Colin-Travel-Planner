# Skye and the West Highlands

*A beach campsite under the Cuillin, and the villages the Clearances emptied*

## Why

The top European pick on Colin's own rules (2026-11-01): a campsite running down to a beach, with showers (where this daydream started), **explorable abandoned croft villages** (`me/principles.md` 10), sunsets on the west coast, and no language barrier. ~9 days **keeps** on PTO.

## Research notes

### 2026-11-01 · First look
- ✅ **Glenbrittle Campsite & Cafe**: 160 pitches spread down to Loch Brittle beach below the Cuillin; shower block, laundry, café; **open 26 March – 15 October**; non-electric pitches are allocated on arrival, no booking *(source: dunvegancastle.com/glenbrittle, 2026-11-01)*
- 📋 **Rubh an Dùnain** from the campsite: 12.8 km there and back, ~4 h with exploring: an Iron Age fort, a Viking canal, a cairn and the abandoned MacAskill croft houses. River crossings can be tricky in high water *(source: isleofskye.com)*
- 📋 Coire Lagan, a lochan in the Cuillin, starts beside the campsite toilet block; Suisnish clearance village is ~10 mi away *(source: britainexpress.com, 2021-11)*
- 📋 **Midges:** late May to September; the worst stretch is the last week of June and the first three weeks of July; effectively over by mid-September. Wind above ~6 mph grounds them *(source: outdoorscot.co.uk, 2026-04; lovefromscotland.co.uk)*. Skye is among the worst places *(source: campfind.co.uk)*
- 📋 Scotland's right to roam covers tent camping on Skye *(source: campfind.co.uk)*
