# Greenbrier Valley

*78 miles of river, two tunnels, and nobody*

## Why

The hidden gem of the bike ideas. 📋 The Greenbrier River Trail is 78 miles *(source: facebook bestpavedcycletrails, search snippet, 2026-09-30)* with 37 bridges and two tunnels, including the 402 ft Droop Mountain Tunnel *(source: greenbrierrivertrail.com, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. 📋 Swimming spots along its length *(source: wvstateparks.com, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. Watoga State Park sits on it as a base. Views are river valley rather than summits; that's the trade.
