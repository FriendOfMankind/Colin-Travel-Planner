## Research notes

### 2026-10-29 · Camping and car rental, first look
- ✅ Campervans, caravans and tent trailers may not stay overnight outside organised campsites or urban areas without the landowner's permission (Nature Conservation Act 60/2013) *(source: ust.is/english/visiting-iceland/travel-information/where-can-you-camp, search result quoting the page, 2026-10-29)*
- ✅ A traditional tent may go on uncultivated land for one night if there's no campsite nearby and no sign prohibits it. Groups of more than three tents need permission *(source: ust.is access-rights page, search result quoting the page, 2026-10-29)*
- 📋 About 170 registered campsites; peak camping season is late May to August *(source: wayfarer.is/en/blog/camping-in-iceland, 2026-07)*
- 📋 A typical night costs 4,900 ISK for two adults with a tent or van, including the 400 ISK lodging tax; half of sites charge 4,400–5,400 ISK. Price barely predicts ratings, and the Westfjords rate best *(source: guidetoiceland.is 128-campsite study, 2026-10-06)*. visiticeland.com says most charge 2,000–3,500 ISK per person
- 📋 Many campsites close mid-to-late September and reopen in May or June; a few stay open all year *(source: guidetoiceland.is, 2026-10-06)*
- 📋 A 2WD is fine for the Ring Road and Golden Circle. F-roads legally need a 4x4, and a 2WD damaged on one isn't insured *(source: northbound.is, guidetoiceland.is, getcarhire.com, 2026)*
- 📋 River-crossing water damage is almost always excluded, even with upgraded insurance. Basic cover often leaves out gravel, wind, and sand-and-ash damage *(source: guidetoiceland.is river-crossing guide, 2026-10; gocarrental.is, 2026-07)*
- 📋 Since 2026-01-01, every vehicle pays a kilometre fee: ISK 6.95/km, or ISK 1,390 per rental day on the daily scheme (based on 200 km/day). It's usually not included in the quoted price *(source: northbound.is help article, 2026-09)*
- 📋 Average daily rental, compact cars and 4x4s, 2026: Jun €124 · Jul €158 · Aug €237 · Sep €180 *(source: northbound.is chart, 2026-10)*. A Hyundai i10 for 14 days costs $622 in Jan–Mar and $1,492 in August *(source: gocarrental.is, 2026-07)*
- 📋 2-person campervans in summer typically run €151–€434/day (median €207); 4x4 campers €221–€489 *(source: campersearch.is, checks through 2026-10-13)*
- 📋 Sprengisandur and Askja, the remotest interior crossings, generally stay shut until July *(source: campersearch.is June page)*
- ⚠️ Car plus his own tent plus campsites should beat a campervan on cost. That's my inference from the figures above, not costed
- ⚠️ Summer nights are probably around his tested floor, the 40s°F (`me/camping.md`), and wind is the bigger threat to a tent. General knowledge, not checked against climate normals
- ⚠️ Whether sleeping in an ordinary rental car counts as a "similar vehicle" under the campsite-only rule isn't settled by the sources read. Assume campsites
