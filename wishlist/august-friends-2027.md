### 2026-10-10 · Shortlist: three fly-in hubs with August water
Each one has a real raft run, swimming, and spots locals know. All three carry the same August risk: **wildfire smoke** (not researched yet).

**1. Columbia Gorge + Oregon (fly PDX)**
- 📋 **White Salmon River**, Class III–IV, with **Husum Falls**: a 12–14 ft drop billed as the tallest commercially rafted waterfall in the US. It's optional, and runnable later in summer (usually from early July) when the flow drops. Half day ~$85–105 per person (riverrider.com; pdxraft.com $88 + tax; explorewashingtonstate.com, snippets, 2026-10-10). Book a week ahead in July–August; weekends sell out.
- 📋 **Deschutes at Maupin**: Class III–IV, and the outfitter stops "to swim and jump off rocks at the great swimming holes" (raftdra.com, snippet).
- 📋 **Punch Bowl Falls** on Eagle Creek: a swimming pool under a 36 ft fall, ~2 mi in, 45 min from Portland (pdxmonthly.com, 2026-06-30). **The Eagle Creek trail reopened in 2021** (oregonhikers.org, snippet), which updates the bucket item.
- Bucket items in Oregon: Eagle Creek to Tunnel Falls, Tamolitch Blue Pool, Sunstone area.

**2. Idaho: Boise → Payette → Stanley (fly BOI)**
- 📋 **South Fork Payette Canyon**: 25 Class III–IV rapids, a portage around Big Falls, and a soak at Pine Flats Hot Springs; $149 full day, 12+ (payetterivercompany.com; cascaderaft.com, snippets). Staircase half day $89. **The South Fork season is listed as May–August**, so late August is the edge of it.
- 📋 **The Kirkham section**: Class II–III, Kirkham Hot Springs and a **jump rock**; half day $65 at 4:30 PM (payetterivercompany.com).
- 📋 **Stanley hot springs**: Boat Box (a tub by the road, 3–4 mi east of Stanley), Sunbeam (pools in the river), Goldbug further out (walkmyworld.com, 2026-04-07). This is the most "I know a spot" region of the three.
- Bucket items in Idaho: Route of the Hiawatha and Emerald Creek garnets are both up north, a separate Spokane-based version, not Boise.

**3. Reno / Tahoe (fly RNO)**
- 📋 **South Fork American**: Class III (Troublemaker, Satan's Cesspool) with swimming and rock jumping between rapids, $67–102 half day (raft-h2o.com, snippet). Tamer than the other two.
- 📋 Tahoe swimming and rock jumping is the marketing image (visitrenotahoe.com). ⚠️ Which jump spots are legal or safe: not checked.
- ⚠️ This is the one that shares a hub with the Eastern Sierra idea, so it could also be a Sierra/Tahoe week.

**Car and age (the hidden constraint)**
- 📋 Hertz rents from age 20, with a young-renter fee of **$25/day** (hertz.com, snippet). Expedia's RNO page warns that **vans and SUVs often have age limits** for under-25s. Five guys plus luggage in a compact won't work. If someone in the group is 25+, they should be the renter (`me/profile.md`: AAA is the usual workaround).

**My pick, so far:** Idaho, for water plus hot springs plus the strongest locals'-spots culture, with the Gorge a close second because of Husum Falls. Not priced, and the smoke risk isn't checked.

- 📋 **Crater Lake swimming is out for 2027**: Cleetwood Cove trail, the only lake access, is closed for the 2026–2028 summers (discoverklamath.com; travel oregon, snippets, 2026-10-10)
- 📋 **Smoke**: southwestern Idaho was under air quality advisories Aug 3–7 2026 (idahonews.com, boisedev.com); Oregon had late-Aug/early-Sept 2025 smoke from the Emigrant Fire (Oregon DEQ). Every route needs a plan B
- 📋 Idaho edge options found: Perrine Bridge in Twin Falls is billed as the only US bridge open to BASE jumping year-round, with tandem jumps sold (visitsouthidaho.com); kayak to Shoshone Falls (212 ft); Dierkes Lake cliff jumps (worldtosarah.blog, 2026-07-22). Tandem BASE is above "fun-scary"
- 📋 Tahoe jump spots: Rooster Rock (D.L. Bliss), Angora Lakes with 10–65 ft ledges, Bonsai Rock (Tahoe Daily Tribune, 2021). Legality not checked
