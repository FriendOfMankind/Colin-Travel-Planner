# Acadia

*The bike trip, if there's only one*

## Why

The best bike-and-hike match in the East for a hybrid. 📋 45 miles of car-free broken-stone carriage roads, for foot, bike and horse only *(source: medium.com "Cycling Acadia's Quiet Roads", search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. That's 3–4 riding days without repeats, and rock-and-ladder hiking days in between. ⚠️ The iron-rung cliff routes (Precipice, Beehive) are the Honey Creek kind of hard: recalled, current status not checked.

## Next

Pick the base campground knowing that 📋 Blackwoods doesn't connect to the carriage roads by bike *(source: tripadvisor and facebook forum threads, search snippets, 2026-09-30)*, and that the Island Explorer bus carries bikes 📋 *(source: acadiaonmymind.com, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*.
