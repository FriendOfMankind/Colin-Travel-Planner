# Pictured Rocks + Grand Island

*Raw, car-free, and ferry-only*

## Why

Grand Island is a ferry ride off Munising and 📋 has a mix of rugged dirt/sand trails and easier gravel roads *(source: munising.org, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. 📋 The loop is 20.6 mi and AllTrails calls it challenging *(source: alltrails.com grand-island-loop, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. Pictured Rocks cliffs are the hiking days: 📋 Chapel loop ~9–10 mi *(source: reddit r/CampAndHikeMichigan, search snippet, 2026-09-30)*. Quiet because it's hard to reach, not because it's bad.
