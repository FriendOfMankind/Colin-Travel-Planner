# Idaho Boys Trip

*Payette whitewater, Stanley hot springs, the Sawtooths and Twin Falls with friends*

## Why

The friends-week route with the best texture of the three: Class IV on the South Fork Payette, Sawtooth Lake, your own inflatable kayak on the Salmon, Custer ghost town, a lava tube, and hot water almost every night. It was my early pick and scored third (38/50), mostly on smoke: 2026 advisories ran Aug 4–12, the exact dates. Brief and group answers: `wishlist/august-friends-2027.md`.

## Next

Nothing for 2027. If it comes back, aim for July, before the smoke peak and inside the South Fork season. Re-price everything (all 2025–26 figures).

### 2026-10-10 · Shortlist notes
**2. Idaho: Boise → Payette → Stanley (fly BOI)**
- 📋 **South Fork Payette Canyon**: 25 Class III–IV rapids, a portage around Big Falls, and a soak at Pine Flats Hot Springs; $149 full day, 12+ (payetterivercompany.com; cascaderaft.com, snippets). Staircase half day $89. **The South Fork season is listed as May–August**, so late August is the edge of it.
- 📋 **The Kirkham section**: Class II–III, Kirkham Hot Springs and a **jump rock**; half day $65 at 4:30 PM (payetterivercompany.com).
- 📋 **Stanley hot springs**: Boat Box (a tub by the road, 3–4 mi east of Stanley), Sunbeam (pools in the river), Goldbug further out (walkmyworld.com, 2026-04-07). This is the most "I know a spot" region of the three.
- Bucket items in Idaho: Route of the Hiawatha and Emerald Creek garnets are both up north, a separate Spokane-based version, not Boise.

- 📋 Idaho edge options found: Perrine Bridge in Twin Falls is billed as the only US bridge open to BASE jumping year-round, with tandem jumps sold (visitsouthidaho.com); kayak to Shoshone Falls (212 ft); Dierkes Lake cliff jumps (worldtosarah.blog, 2026-07-22). Tandem BASE is above "fun-scary"
- 📋 **Smoke**: southwestern Idaho was under air quality advisories Aug 3–7 2026 (idahonews.com, boisedev.com); Oregon had late-Aug/early-Sept 2025 smoke from the Emigrant Fire (Oregon DEQ). Every route needs a plan B
