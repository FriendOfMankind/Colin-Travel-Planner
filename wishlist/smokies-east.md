# Great Smokies — the east side

*Where it stops being a traffic jam*

## Why

⚠️ Cataloochee, Big Creek, Balsam Mountain and Deep Creek — the east side is the crowd escape. Mount Cammerer and Ramsey Cascades are the day hikes; Gregory Bald is a June thing. ⚠️ **Late** April, not early — the higher country and some roads are still shut at the start of the month.
