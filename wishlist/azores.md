## Research notes

### 2026-11-01 · First look
- 📋 Azores Airlines flies nonstop from Boston to Ponta Delgada (São Miguel) all year; JFK and Newark (United) are seasonal; ~5 h *(source: travel-azores.com, 2026-03; azoresgetaways.com)*. Ryanair stopped flying to the Azores in March 2026 *(same)*
- 📋 São Miguel has five camping places, including Furnas (hot springs) and Sete Cidades (crater lake); camping outside them isn't allowed in the protected areas *(source: byacores.com, 2026-06)*
- 📋 On **Flores every campsite is free**. Fajã Grande has drinkable water and hot showers, by the Ribeira do Ferreiro waterfall *(source: easyhikeazores.com)*
- 📋 Flores rental cars are scarce and pricey: one traveller paid €900 for 7 days in September vs €370 for 15 days on Terceira *(source: toazoresislands.com, 2024-10)*. Flores is reached only by inter-island flights
