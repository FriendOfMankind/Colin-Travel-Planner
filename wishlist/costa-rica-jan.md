# Costa Rica Road Trip

*A car, a crew, jungle wildlife, hot springs and a Guanacaste surf town*

## Why

The easier, pricier alternative to `wishlist/guatemala-el-salvador-jan.md`, from the same brief: ~10 days in January with the Adirondacks crew, beach/surf for them, no Spanish, nobody 25+ *(stated 2026-11-03)*. Colin: "we don't NEED hard hiking, if Costa Rica is more wildlife and jungle and beach oriented so be it" *(stated 2026-11-03)*.

What it buys: your own car (a road trip, closest to how Colin already travels), lots of English, wildlife without trying, hot springs, Río Celeste, and a surf town at the end. Flights land at Liberia (LIR), next to Guanacaste.

What it doesn't have: **no volcano summits in the north are open** (notes below), and no erupting volcano to watch. If the crew wants that night, it's the Guatemala idea.

**Paid outings:** a canopy zipline is fine *with friends, if high value* *(stated 2026-11-03)*. `me/declined.md` still lists zipline tickets (written about New River Gorge), so that entry needs narrowing through `/remember` before one goes in a plan.

## The plan (Jan 7–16, 10 days, 9 nights)

Colin's pick, 2026-11-03: Costa Rica over Guatemala + El Salvador. **Airbnb houses**; the Arenal base is **El Castillo**; the cave is **Venado only** *(all stated 2026-11-03)*. Crew size **unknown yet**, so the pitch prices 3 and 4.

**Every day ends before dark** (~6 pm, *liberiacostarica.com*). Houses have kitchens: cook breakfasts and most dinners, eat out for a named dish (principle 9). Costa Rican food is mild, inside the spice ceiling. Two shops (principle 9b): the preliminary shop in Liberia on day 1, the final one in Tamarindo on day 6.

**Festivals avoided:** **Fiestas Típicas Nacionales in Santa Cruz, Jan 14–18, 2027** (tope from noon, bull riding 2–8 pm, dianas from 5 am), 20–30 min inland from Tamarindo, overlap our beach days. So: no Santa Cruz roads Jan 14–18, and the airport run goes via Huacas–Filadelfia. Nicoya's fiestas (~Jan 14–25) are why the Barra Honda cave was dropped. Palmares (late Jan) is in the Central Valley, off the route. Dreamsea Sónica (Tamarindo, Jan 22–23) is after we leave. Dec 24 – Jan 6 is the peak crush, dodged by the dates. ## Warnings

- **Rip currents.** State Dept calls them "very dangerous". Never swim alone or after drinking; Playa Grande's drop-off is the worst *(nicoyapeninsula.com)*.
- **No driving after dark.** The Lake Arenal road is narrow, curvy and unlit; dark is around 6 pm *(liberiacostarica.com)*.
- **Car break-ins.** Nothing left in the car, especially at Río Celeste.
- **Las Pailas can close without notice** in eruptive phases (it stayed open through 2026's). Fallback: a longer hot-springs morning.

## Research notes

### 2026-11-03 · First pass: volcanoes, rental, safety, flights
- ✅ **Costa Rica: Level 2, Exercise increased caution** (crime), issued 2026-04-02. Petty crime common; violent crime also affects tourists; rip currents "very dangerous" *(source: travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/costa-rica.html, 2026-11-03)*. Warning-grade for the surf leg: never swim alone, never after drinking
- 📋 **Arenal:** summit permanently closed; **Cerro Chato** (the crater-lake hike) closed since 2017 for recovery, still closed as of April 2026; unofficial private routes are not sanctioned. Open trails are base loops (~2–4 km) over the 1968 lava fields. Entry ~$15; 8:00–16:00; clearest views 8–10 am *(source: costarica-spirit.com/guides/arenal-volcano-national-park, 2026-07, snippet)*. ⚠️ arenal.net still describes Cerro Chato as a "climbable trail" (visitcostarica.com FAQ, 2026-05). The two sources disagree; the closure source is newer and specific
- 📋 **Rincón de la Vieja:** summit trail closed since 2013 *(source: frommers.com, snippet)*; the SINAC page says summit access depends on weather and volcanic conditions *(✅ sinac.go.cr, pnrv page, 2026-11-03)*. Las Pailas sector: Tue–Sun 8:00–15:00, closed Mondays, no camping. **Santa María sector campground: "Actualmente cerrado"** *(✅ sinac.go.cr, 2026-11-03)*. Entry ~US$16.95, sold online only, plus a Hacienda Guachipelín toll *(source: viajacongus.com, "verified July 2026", snippet)*
- 📋 **Rental:** minimum age 21 at most majors (Sixt 18; Adobe 23, no exceptions); 21–24 pay ~$10/day at Enterprise/National *(source: enterprise.cr requirements guide; nationalcar.cr FAQ, snippets)*, $10–20/day elsewhere. An older friend booking doesn't avoid it: additional drivers pay the same fee *(source: rentalverdict.com, 2026-06, snippet)*. Mandatory liability insurance can't be waived by a credit card and roughly doubles the teaser rates: ~$30–55/day economy, $60–110 4×4, all-in *(source: journeyfinder.net, 2026-07; expatfocus.com, snippets)*. Peak season Dec–Apr is the most expensive *(thoroughlytravel.com, snippet)*
- 📋 **Flights:** CLE ↔ LIR round trips seen around $390 with one stop *(source: KAYAK route page, 2026-09, snippet)*; December is high season *(same)*. Not a January quote
- 📋 Dry season: ~Dec–Apr in Guanacaste. Arenal's own rainy season runs "roughly May to January" *(source: arenal.net, 2026-09, snippet)*, so January at Arenal can still be wet

### 2026-11-03 · Plan and cost pass
- 📋 Dec 24 – Jan 6 is one of the two worst crowding periods at Guanacaste beaches; hotels book out months ahead *(source: costarica-spirit.com/guides/guanacaste-beach-guide, 2026-07, snippet)*. The first week of January stays busy, then eases to normal high season *(mytanfeet.com, snippet)*
- 📋 CLE ↔ LIR: typical $444–604 round trip, January average ~$560, no nonstop *(momondo route page, 2026-09)*; typical $571–889 *(KAYAK route page, 2026-09)*. One-stop via DFW, ORD or Toronto
- 📋 Drive times: LIR → Tamarindo 1 h 15 – 1 h 30; LIR → Las Pailas 45 min – 1 h 15; Rincón ↔ Río Celeste ~2 – 2 h 15; Río Celeste is "not a day trip" from Tamarindo *(liberiacostarica.com/guanacaste-drive-times, snippet)*. La Fortuna → Río Celeste 1 h 30, paved *(rio-celeste.org/directions, 2026-08)*. La Fortuna → Tamarindo area ~3½–4 h *(costaricaprivateresorts.com, 2026-10)*
- 📋 Río Celeste: $12 non-resident entry *(junglelifecr.com)*; tickets online only, 40-minute entry windows 8:00–13:45, slots sell out in dry season; parking ₡3,000 *(rio-celeste.org, 2026-08)*
- 📋 Las Pailas: 3 km loop ~2 h; entry before noon in dry season; closed Mondays *(✅ sinac.go.cr pnrv page, 2026-11-03)*. Entry $15 + 13% tax; crater route closed through the 2026 eruptions; Las Pailas stayed open, checked 2026-08-12 *(volcano.travel, snippet)*. travel-surf.com (2026-10) also notes the 2026 eruption
- 📋 La Fortuna: waterfall $18, ~500 steps *(arenal.net, 2026-09)*; Río Chollín hot river free; Baldi $40; Tabacón full day $105 *(Frommer's; rainforesttours.com)*; night tours $45–90 *(Frommer's; Viator)*
- 📋 Leatherbacks at Playa Grande (Las Baulas NP): nesting Oct–Feb, peak Nov–Jan; tours ~$85–100 incl. ~$18 park fee; book ahead Dec–Jan; no guarantee *(costarica-spirit.com, 2026-07; ✅ sinac.go.cr Las Baulas page)*
- 📋 Tamarindo surf: group lesson $45–70, board $20–25/day *(travel-surf.com, 2025-10; nativeswaycostarica.com)*; Dec–Apr waves 1–4 ft, the beginner window *(waverick.com, 2026-06)*
- 📋 Lodging: La Fortuna Airbnb high-season nightly ~₡69,600 *(airroi.com, 2026-10)*; Tamarindo peak ADR $428–556, skewed by large villas *(airroi.com, 2026-09)*; KAYAK lists Tamarindo villas and houses from ~$110–230 *(2026-10)*; La Fortuna hostel private rooms ~$25 *(budgetyourtrip.com, 2026-10)*

### 2026-11-03 · Day plan: festivals, gems, sleep and food
- 📋 **Fiestas Típicas Nacionales, Santa Cruz: Jan 14–18, 2027**, announced by the organisers in Oct 2026; 2026's ran the same dates, with a tope de toros from noon and montaderas 2–8 pm daily, dianas from 5 am Jan 15–18 *(guanacasteexperiences.com events calendar and blog, 2026-10; Periódico Mensaje, 2026-01-09; salsavida.com)*
- 📋 Nicoya's Fiestas Cívicas ~Jan 14–25 ("dates can vary") *(nicoyapeninsula.com, 2026-01)*; another source puts San Blas in early Feb *(guanacasteexperiences.com)*. The two disagree; either way it's why Barra Honda was skipped
- 📋 Fiestas de Palmares, second half of January (Jan 15–26 in 2026) *(midlifenomads.com, 2026-10)*: Central Valley, off the route. Dreamsea Sónica, Tamarindo, Jan 22–23, 2027 *(guanacasteexperiences.com)*: after departure. Liberia's fiestas are late Feb – early Mar
- 📋 **Llanos de Cortés:** near Bagaces, just off Route 1; 8:00–16:00, last entry 15:30; $7 foreign adult, cash; ~3–5 min walk on a concrete path; "not as hidden as it used to be" *(puravidatraveling.com, 2026-06; mytanfeet.com, 2025-08)*
- 📋 **Venado Caves:** limestone cave with an underground river, crawling, bats, marine fossils; 1.5 h; $99 regular incl. transport and gear *(arenalexpeditions.com, 2025-11)*; Venado is ~45 min from La Fortuna *(casavenado.com)*
- 📋 **Arenal 1968:** $25 adult *(vamosrentacar.com, 2026-01)*; El Silencio ~$10 for the same lava flow; Arenal Observatory Lodge lunch with a view called "one of La Fortuna's hidden gems" *(earthtrekkers.com, 2025-05)*. El Castillo is reached by the dirt road off Route 142 *(vamosrentacar.com)*
- 📋 Lake Arenal road via Nuevo Arenal and Tilarán: "some of the most" scenic driving in the area *(casavenado.com)*; La Fortuna ↔ Tilarán road in good condition *(arenal.net)*. Old Arenal and Tronadora were relocated in 1979, and remnants are under the lake *(twoweeksincostarica.com)*
- 📋 **La Cangreja/Escondida waterfall trails** at Las Pailas: entry before 12:00; tickets on the SINAC site with passport details, card only *(cantomar.com, 2026-08)*
- 📋 Barra Honda: vertical-ladder cave near Nicoya *(visitcostarica.com)*. Considered, not picked (Colin chose Venado only, 2026-11-03)
- 📋 La Leona waterfall and Poza Los Coyotes near Curubandé appear in tour listings *(viator.com)*. ⚠️ independent access and guide rules not checked
