# Adirondacks Ski Week

*Lake Placid in January with friends*

## Why

Colin asked for winter activities that are "realistic and affordable and fun for me to pull off this winter", as a non-skier, with something to slide down after *(stated 2026-10-15)*. Lake Placid has all of it within 20 minutes: Whiteface for the friends who ski, beginner lessons for Colin, Olympic cross-country trails, a bobsled ride that needs no skill, cheap lit night XC, and a toboggan chute onto Mirror Lake.

**It's a friends trip.** Colin "could get someone to come", friends who ski, and "as long as they get a day or two to ski it'll be worth it" *(stated 2026-10-15)*. Group: **3–4 including Colin, all students**, free any time after Jan 4 *(stated 2026-10-15)*. With friends, winter High Peaks stop breaking the solo rule in `me/hiking.md` ("I don't wanna get lost alone"); solo, Cascade in winter would.

**Horizon: keeps.** A 5-day winter drive trip is easy on PTO after 2027-08-31. It's here because January is otherwise empty (`me/calendar.md`: Frisco ends Dec 30, spring term starts Jan 19), not because it's now-or-never. Snow trips also already cover December (Frisco), which is an argument for keeping spring break as the desert trip (`trips/sky-islands-2027/trip.md`).

**Cold:** `me/camping.md` records comfort to the 40s. That's why this is a cabin, not camping.

Dated entries, newest last. What was found while daydreaming, with a source on every fact. `/daydream` writes here.

### 2026-10-15 · Lake Placid in January
- ✅ Whiteface holiday dates for 2026/27: **Dec 25, 2026–Jan 1, 2027; Jan 16–18, 2027; Feb 13–21, 2027** *(whiteface.com/lesson/group-lessons-2, 2026-10-15)*
- ✅ Whiteface **Learn to Ski/Snowboard, levels 1–3** (lift ticket + 2 h lesson + rental, 13+): **$189 midweek non-holiday, $209 weekend/holiday**. Group lesson only: $109 *(same page, 2026/27 rates)*
- 📋 Whiteface lift tickets: not found on the page. The Nov 24–Dec 3 2025 Cyber Sale sold a **2-pack of non-holiday tickets for $174** *(whiteface.com/event/cyber-sale-2025, search snippet)*; whether there's a 2026 sale is unknown. An aggregator says "from $79" for 2026/27 *(snowstash.com, snippet; not trusted)*. Whiteface says buying online early is cheapest
- 📋 Mt Van Hoevenberg: 44 km of XC trails, adult pass **$32/day**, Nordic ski or snowshoe rental **$25/day**, open daily 9–5 *(mtvanhoevenberg.com, 2026-10-15; that page also showed last season as "Closed for the Season", so rates may change)*
- 📋 **Bobsled Experience**: pro driver and brakeman, from the half-mile point *(visitadirondacks.com)*; booked by request form, up to 6 per order *(mtvanhoevenberg.com)*. **Price not found**
- 📋 **Lake Placid Toboggan Chute**: 30 ft chute onto Mirror Lake, $20 adult / **$10 student**, unlimited rides per session, card only, on-site sales only, needs **12 in of ice** *(lakeplacid.com, 2026-10-15)*. Runs weekends, "usually starting in January" *(highpeaksresort.com FAQ, snippet)*. ⚠️ So Jan 9–10 is the riskiest weekend for it
- 📋 **Dewey Mountain**, Saranac Lake: day pass **$5**, rental **$18/day** *(saranaclake.com)*; after Jan 1 open Mon–Fri 10–7 with lit lower trails; **Tuesday is race night** *(deweymountain.com/hours--info.html)*
- 📋 Mirror Lake: a free ~2 mi plowed skating track once the ice is thick *(highpeaksresort.com FAQ, snippet)*
- ⚠️ Mt Jo (short, big view over Heart Lake) and Cascade (classic first winter High Peak, exposed summit): from memory, not checked. lakeplacid.com advises a licensed guide for new winter hikers
- ⚠️ Avalanche and route-finding: not researched for Cascade in January

### 2026-10-15 · Where to stay
- Colin found Airbnbs at **~$350 pp for the week** *(stated 2026-10-15)*
- 📋 Wilmington is the base pick: closest to Whiteface (listings say 2–15 min), ~20 min to Lake Placid. 2BR cabins listed at $335–463/night, dates not shown *(sun-ski.com, snippet)*
- 📋 Adirondak Loj (ADK, Heart Lake): 38 guests, private rooms, 6-person bunkrooms and a loft, shared baths, breakfast included, open year-round, no cell service, Mt Jo from the door *(adk.org, 2026-10-15)*. Bunkrooms "$81–91" *(tenereteam.com, third-party, unclear if per bed)*
- 📋 Motels: Quality Inn Lake Placid ~$129/night; Adirondack Spruce Lodge (Wilmington, suites for up to 6) "from $149" *(nyhotels.net, dates not shown)*
- ⚠️ Ask any host: is the driveway plowed, does it need AWD? Whether the Legacy has winter tires: not recorded. Some hosts block under-25 groups
