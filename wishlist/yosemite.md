# Yosemite

*The icons at full roar, with somewhere empty to escape to*

## Why

⭐ **A lifetime must** *(stated 2026-10-29: "I'll definitely want to visit Yosemite sometime in my life")*. The plan is built around his new crowd rule (`me/travel-style.md`): the world-class icons are worth their crowds, as long as the trip also has empty places to retreat to. Zion in May is the model: busy, unbelievable, and the e-bike day was "awesome" *(stated 2026-10-29)*.

**Why late May to mid-June, not September.** This is when the waterfalls peak. Yosemite Falls peaks in May, and Nevada Fall in late May or June *(📋 nps.gov/yose waterfalls page, search excerpt, 2026-10-29)*. By September they're trickles. The cost is that Tioga Road may still be snowed shut. It opened May 15 in 2026 *(📋 yosemite.org, 2026)*. The long-run average is about May 28, the 2010s averaged May 23, and the latest opening on record is July 8 *(✅ monobasinresearch.org Tioga dates table, 1933–2022)*. So the plan has two versions: east over Tioga, or back out west.

**A group trip: Colin + 2–3 friends** *(stated 2026-10-29)*. Splitting the rental car is the money reason, and late May to June is a window when people can join. That fits the rule in `me/adventures.md` that guided outings come with friends, so the Merced rafting day moves from option to likely. One Valley campsite holds the whole group: max **6 people and 2 vehicles** per site *(📋 nps.gov campground regulations; recreation.gov Upper Pines site pages)*, at $36 a night split four ways.

**It keeps.** Nine days fits two weeks of PTO in any year, so this isn't a last-free-summer trip. Late May to June 2027 is already Newfoundland (`trips/newfoundland-2027/`), so the earliest real slot is 2028.

**Related ideas.** In a Tioga-open year, `wishlist/eastern-sierra.md` is right over the pass. That idea is July to October, so pairing the two means a June version of it. `wishlist/sequoia-kings.md` is the other big park to the south.

## Draft plan (outline, late May to early June, 9 days)

An outline, not a schedule: no times, and drives are estimates unless sourced. One outing a day, with long sits built in.

**Getting there.** Fly into the Bay Area (SFO/OAK/SJC) and rent a car. If Tioga is open, fly home from Reno, an open-jaw nobody has priced yet. Fresno is the closest airport to the south entrance. Pre-buy the park pass on recreation.gov to get through the gate faster *(📋 Evergreen Lodge travel page, 2026)*. With 3–4 people plus a trunk camp, a sedan won't hold it: rent an SUV or minivan, and price the Reno one-way drop fee against round-tripping the Bay Area.

**Sleeping.** There's **no first-come camping inside Yosemite** *(📋 Yosemite Guide, 2025)*. You also can't sleep in a vehicle unless you're registered for a site *(📋 nps.gov/yose camping page, 2026-09)*. The options:
- **Valley campgrounds** (Upper/Lower/North Pines, $36): released five months ahead on the 15th at 7 am PT, and they "sell out within minutes" *(📋 nps.gov, 2026)*. The upside of a Valley site is real: you wake up inside the crowd-free hours with no parking hunt, which is what makes slow mornings possible here. **For late May to June, aim at Upper Pines.** Flood-prone sites in North and Lower Pines aren't released until the river is safe, often "just a few days" ahead *(📋 nps.gov campground FAQ)*. With a group, several people can be logged in at 7 am, but each visit allows only two reservations, and the primary occupant shows photo ID at check-in *(📋 nps.gov; recreation.gov)*. Cancelling a spare costs $10 if done more than 48 h out *(📋 recreation.gov)*.
- **Camp 4** ($10 per person, shared sites): reserved one week ahead from mid-April *(📋 nps.gov/yose camp4, 2026)*. Climber history, and a social, solo-friendly camp.
- **Hodgdon Meadow** (near the Hwy 120 entrance) and **Wawona**: same five-month rule.
- **Free dispersed camping outside the park**: Hardin Flat Road on the Hwy 120 corridor, and Goat Meadow near Fish Camp *(📋 thetalusfieldjournal.com, 2026-07)*. No water, toilets or lockers. Stanislaus NF limit is 21 days per district, and you park within one vehicle length of the road *(✅ Stanislaus NF dispersed factsheet, 2026-08)*.
- Valley limit: 7 nights between May 1 and Sep 15 *(📋 nps.gov, 2026)*.

**Days, Tioga-open version:**
1. **Fly in, drive to the Hwy 120 side.** Stock the trunk in the Central Valley. Camp at Hodgdon or Hardin Flat.
2. **Hetch Hetchy.** Wapama Falls, 5.0 mi / 928 ft, Moderate *(✅ AllTrails)*. It's a 1,400 ft fall that peaks in May, and "relatively few people visit" *(📋 nps.gov)*. Tueeulala Falls next to it dries up by early summer. The Hetch Hetchy road is open sunrise to sunset only *(📋 nps.gov)*. ⚠️ See the footbridge warning below.
3. **First Valley day, on a bike.** Tunnel View on the way in. Rent a bike (Curry Village, Yosemite Village or the Lodge, first-come, about $48/day *(📋 redtailebikes.com citing travelyosemite, 2026)*) and ride the loop: Cook's Meadow, Swinging Bridge, Stoneman Meadow boardwalk, Lower Yosemite Fall in full spray. Then lock the bike and walk up to Mirror Lake, which is fullest in spring (rental bikes aren't allowed on the Mirror Lake hill). Finish by lying on the big log in El Capitan Meadow. Tunnel View at sunset.
4. **The big one: Glacier Point bus up, Panorama Trail down.** Ride the tour bus from Yosemite Valley Lodge to Glacier Point one-way, then hike 8.2 mi with a net 3,200 ft descent past Illilouette, Nevada and Vernal Falls *(📋 nps.gov; bus began May 9 in 2026, "drop-off only, no pickup at Glacier Point")*. That's every big waterfall in one day, mostly downhill. There's one ~800 ft climb after Illilouette *(📋 nps.gov)*; the rest is descent, so it's hard on knees, not lungs. Finish down the Mist Trail's stone steps, or the JMT if your knees complain.
5. **Rest day, near-empty Valley.** The west half of the Valley Loop Trail ("rarely see another human being", 📋 yosemite.com). Meadows, birding, a long sit. Late afternoon, drive up to Sentinel Dome + Taft Point, a 5.4 mi / 1,066 ft loop *(✅ AllTrails)*, and stay for sunset on Sentinel Dome.
6. **Option day, pick one:** Upper Yosemite Falls, 7.1 mi / 3,169 ft, Hard *(✅ AllTrails)*. It's over your 2,500 ft ceiling, so it has to earn it. Columbia Rock is a turnaround about a mile up *(📋 Yosemite Guide)*. **Or** guided Merced whitewater, Class III–IV, from ~$190 *(📋 ARTA)*, meeting about 45 min from the Valley, season April to early June. Per `me/adventures.md`, a guided outing is a bring-a-friend option.
7. **Tioga Road east.** Olmsted Point, Tenaya Lake, Tuolumne Meadows. Expect snow and flooded trails up high this early *(📋 nps.gov seasons page)*, so keep it to short walks. Over Tioga Pass to Mono Lake's South Tufa at sunset. Camp on the east side.
8. **East side.** Bodie, or Little Lakes Valley (`bucket.yaml`). Join the `eastern-sierra` idea here if the trip runs longer.
9. **Drive to Reno and fly home.**

**Tioga-closed version:** swap days 7–9 for **Wawona**. Chilnualna Falls is 8.2 mi / 2,400 ft. It peaks around May and is "practically deserted compared to the Mist Trail" *(📋 nps.gov; yosemitehikes.com)*, though it's right at your ceiling. Add the Mariposa Grove of sequoias, then fly out from Fresno or the Bay Area.

**⚠️ Warnings: things that can hurt you or kill the day**
- **Wapama Falls footbridges.** At peak flow, the westernmost bridge can go underwater. Hikers were swept off and killed in 2011 (two) and 2017 (one) *(📋 NPS parkplanning project page)*. A rerouted bridge was proposed; whether it's built isn't known. If water is over the bridge, turn around.
- **Bears vs. the trunk.** Federal law: by day, food stays hidden in a locked car with windows shut. At night it goes in a locker *(📋 Yosemite Guide 2026)*. Bears break into cars. Your "camp lives in the trunk" setup is exactly what this rule targets, so every in-park campsite night means unloading the food into the locker. Dispersed sites have no lockers. The Forest Service food rule there wasn't found. Ask, or rent a canister (Yosemite Basecamp in Groveland rents "bear-aware" gear, 📋). Colin: "I'm good with bears, I'll keep stuff in my car" *(stated 2026-10-29)*. That works **by day** only. Overnight in Yosemite, the law requires the campsite's food locker (every Valley site has one, 📋 recreation.gov North Pines page), and a car is the one place it isn't allowed.
- **Swiftwater.** Spring rivers and creeks are the park's real killer (NPS runs swiftwater rescue videos for Mirror Lake's rock-hops, 📋). Stay out of the Merced at peak.

**Hidden corners worth knowing**
- **Moonbows:** on nights around the full moon in spring, a lunar rainbow forms in the spray. The footbridge at Lower Yosemite Fall is the easiest spot *(📋 yosemite.com)*. Moon dates get computed when a year is set, never typed by hand.
- **The back way to Mirror Lake:** a dirt path from Yosemite Falls behind the Ahwahnee. "No one's ever on it… hidden waterfalls back there" *(✅ YouTube "Best Tips for Visiting Yosemite Without Stress!", uploaded 2025-05-27, recalling a route an old-timer told the guest)*.
- **Ask-a-Climber:** free spotting scopes on El Capitan, west side of El Cap bridge *(📋 Yosemite Guide 2025–26)*. Bring the new binoculars.
- **Grouse Creek Falls, Wildcat Falls and the Cascades** along Hwy 140, roaring in spring *(✅ YouTube FlyingDawnMarie, 2025-05-05, filmed Apr 28–May 2)*.
- **Peregrine falcons:** climbing routes close March 1 to July 15 to protect them *(📋 nps.gov conditions)*. It's a birding target on El Cap and the Valley walls.

## Research notes

Dated entries, newest last. What was found while daydreaming, with a source on every fact. `/daydream` writes here.

### 2026-10-29 · First deep sweep (May–June plan)
- **E-biking like Zion: honestly, not needed here.** Zion's e-bike day works because the canyon road climbs for miles and is closed to cars. Yosemite Valley's paths are "flat" *(✅ travelyosemite.com)*, and the park's rental stands only rent standard bikes *(📋 redtailebikes.com citing travelyosemite, 2026)*. A regular rental bike is the frequent-visitor move: "half-day is plenty… 8 to 10 miles" *(✅ YouTube Keanu Travels To, "BIKING in Yosemite Valley", 2025-02-25, filmed in May)*. If you want the motor anyway, Pedal Forward in Oakhurst has step-through e-bikes with a rack *(✅)*, and Rush Creek Lodge on Hwy 120 rents hybrids at $40/day that "venture into Yosemite" *(📋 rushcreeklodge.com)*.
- **Frequent-visitor patterns** *(✅ YouTube podcast "Best Tips for Visiting Yosemite Without Stress!", 2025-05-27)*: park once and use bike or shuttle, ride the YARTS bus in from gateway towns to skip parking, sit on the log in El Capitan Meadow, Taft Point or Sentinel Dome over Glacier Point itself, Four Mile up and Panorama down for strong hikers, and poppy hillsides plus Merced rafting just outside the park in spring.
- **Bike tips** *(✅ Keanu Travels To, 2025)*: ride in the morning before lines, download offline maps since signal is weak, lock the bike because thefts happen, and stay on paths rather than Northside/Southside Drive.
- **Spring conditions** *(✅ FlyingDawnMarie, 2025-05-05)*: Bridalveil "full force… be prepared to get soaked"; "crowds still fairly minimal this spring… expect Yosemite to get pretty busy by the end of the month."
- **Contradiction, Glacier Point bus:** yosemitehikes.com (undated) says you can sometimes ride the bus *down* from Glacier Point. The 2026 Yosemite Guide says "drop-off only, NO pickup at Glacier Point". The newer official line wins, so plan on riding up and hiking down.
- 2026 trail notes that may or may not persist: the Valley Loop's El Cap bridge was closed on weekdays through Dec 2026, and the Mist Trail had weekday repair closures *(📋 nps.gov conditions)*.
