# Keys Boys Week

*A nice waterfront house with a pool and a dock in Marathon, two boats for the week, and nothing else booked*

## Why

Colin's brief: "the ultimate hedonistic live like kings, boys trip… pure stupid fun luxurious we made it feeling. Zero stress… do whatever we want for like a week" *(stated 2026-11-15)*. Then: **7+ guys, under $2,000 pp with flights, boat life and adventure-luxe, parties among ourselves** *(stated 2026-11-15)*. Then **no staff needed, "just a nice place in a nice location"; tight sleeping quarters are fine as long as everything is nice** *(stated 2026-11-15)*.

It's a planned break from "sleep cheap in an expensive-beautiful place" (`me/travel-style.md`): here the house is the point. Every expensive part (house, boats) is a group cost, so it gets cheap per head with 10–14 guys. A dock behind the house makes "boat life" every day instead of one tour, which is the "do whatever, whenever" part. On the horizon test it **keeps**: a week like this works fine on PTO (`me/calendar.md`).

**Ruled out on the way here** (2026-11-15): a crewed catamaran (~$3,400 pp before tip and flights, double the budget); a staffed Mexican villa (only pencils out at ~14 guys); Roatán, Ambergris Caye, Guanacaste and Bocas del Toro were floated from recall as the Central American alternatives, unpriced. Keys won on ease: no passports, one airport, a boat parked behind the house.

## The week (sketch, 2026-11-15)

One anchor a day, the rest empty. Built for Sat Mar 6 → Sun Mar 14; slides to any Sat-to-Sat week.

1. **Sat · Land and stock up.** CLE → MIA, cars, one big grocery and booze run, drive down (~2–3 h ⚠️), check in, pool.
2. **Sun · Boat shakedown.** Boats delivered or picked up; sandbar, swim, sunset on the dock.
3. **Mon · Reef day.** Snorkel Sombrero Reef ⚠️ in the calm morning; dinner out for a named dish (fish, or stone crab if in season ⚠️).
4. **Tue · Jet ski tour,** then the pool. Key lime pie at some point.
5. **Wed · Do nothing.** No anchor. Protect it.
6. **Thu · Bahia Honda + Key West night.** A sober driver back (~1 h dark highway ⚠️), or rooms. **First cut.**
7. **Fri · Last big boat day,** or a half-day fishing charter (**second cut**). Last-night cookout.
8. **Sat · Checkout (~9 AM on Sat-turnover houses), boats back, one night in Miami** as the closer.
9. **Sun · Fly home.**

**Warnings:** boating drunk is a crime in Florida (BUI); whoever drives the boat that day stays sober, rotate. Sun on day one burns Ohio skin and costs the week.

### 2026-11-15 · Houses, boats, jet skis
- 📋 **Keys houses with a private pool and boat slip, sleeping 12–14:** ~$700–1,000/night in Marathon and Islamorada, many Sat-to-Sat weekly only. One Key Colony Beach house (sleeps 10, pool, slip): $550/night flat Jan 3 – May 1 2027, $300 cleaning, 7-night minimum, check-in Sat 4 PM, out Sat 9 AM *(source: floridarentals.com, search snippets, 2026-11-15)*
- 📋 **Puerto Vallarta, for comparison:** a 6-bedroom villa with heated pool and hot tub, 4.99 over 73 reviews, $2,488 all-in for 3 nights with 12 guests in Feb 2027 *(source: airbnb.com listing, search snippet, 2026-11-15)*
- 📋 **Boats:** 6-person center console, $400/day or $1,900/week, Lower Keys; operators must have boating experience *(source: renttheboatnow.com, snippet, 2026-11-15)*. 18'6" Mako center console weekly from $1,300, Islamorada *(source: thekeysboatrental.com, snippet)*. Key West pontoon $300 half day / $375 full day, ages 25+ *(source: sunsetwatersports.info, snippet)*. **12 guys need two boats** (~$3,800/week ⚠️, plus fuel, unpriced)
- 📋 **Jet skis, Marathon:** "from $99 for two people", 30 min to 2 h rides or tours *(source: marathonjetskirentals.com, snippet, 2026-11-15)*; renters 18+ *(source: nautihookerwatersports.com, snippet)*
- 📋 **Florida boater ID:** anyone born after Jan 1, 1988 needs one to operate a boat *(source: nautihookerwatersports.com, snippet, 2026-11-15)*. The whole group falls under it: 1–2 captains get the card before the trip
- ⚠️ **Booking age:** many rentals want the booker to be 25+ (recall, not checked). Colin is under 25 (`me/profile.md`)
- ⚠️ Read every listing for its party/event rules, max-guest cap, real **bed** count, and whether a boat fits at the dock
- ⚠️ Areas, from recall: **Marathon** (middle, most dock-and-pool houses, best value), Islamorada (nicer, pricier, closer to Miami), Lower Keys (quiet, near Looe Key), Key West (day-trip it, don't stay)
