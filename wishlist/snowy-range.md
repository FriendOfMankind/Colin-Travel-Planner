# Snowy Range / Medicine Bow

*The sleeper of the whole list*

## Why

✅ 29 paved miles topping out above 10,000 ft at Libby Flats, a 12,013 ft peak inside the day-hike ceiling, campgrounds above 10,000 ft, and no hiking permits of any kind. A fraction of Colorado's crowds two hours north of Denver.

✅ **Corrected Sept 6 2026 — this said "mid-July, once the high road opens", and the road was never the constraint.** **WY-130 over Libby Flats opened around May 7 in 2026**, fifteen days earlier than the year before, and in the three years before that it opened by late May. What actually gates the trip is where you sleep: **Brooklyn Lake Campground opens around July 4** and runs to mid-October, and the high-elevation campgrounds are snow-affected through June. For a car-camping trip the camps are the season, so **early July is the honest start** — a week earlier than this entry used to claim, and for a completely different reason.
