## Research notes

### 2026-10-14 · The shortlist, and why the Atlantic North won July
- Shortlist: **Balkans** (Herzegovina–Montenegro loop), **Portugal**, **Northern Spain**. Japan was set aside: August is ~90°F+ and humid, typhoon season, and Obon crowds (⚠️ general knowledge, not checked). Better on PTO in May or October.
- **The Balkans loop** (Split in, Dubrovnik out: Kravica, Mostar, Sarajevo, Tara canyon, Durmitor, Kotor) was the clear #2. It's the most authentic per dollar: Bosnia mid-range runs €70–110/day (📋 bosnia-spirit.com, 2026). Rafting: Una ~€47 + €8 park entry (📋 japodskiotoci.ba); Tara ~$85 (📋 GetYourGuide). The Sarajevo cable car climbs 500 m up Trebević to the graffiti-covered 1984 Olympic bobsled track (📋 Lonely Planet).
- **What sank the Balkans in July:** the heat (Mostar 41°C); Kravica is "extremely busy" in July and August (📋 travelersitch.com, 2026-07); and Croatian agencies often refuse one-way international drop-offs, with a cross-border fee of €38–85 (📋 avaxrent, mack-rent, avantcar). It only works as a loop starting and ending in Croatia, which leaves Slovenia out. **If the trip were in June, the Balkans would be the pick.**
- **Portugal dropped to last for July:** the Fishermen's Trail runs September to June (✅ rotavicentina.com), and one operator calls July–August "hard going, both for the heat and because the villages fill up" (📋 natour.travel). Swimming or self-guided entry into Benagil cave has been banned since July 2024 (📋 benagil.pt). A wildfire started in Peneda-Gerês on Jul 26 2025 (📋 Wikipedia). Porto stays in as the closing city.
- **Two errors in my earlier pitches, corrected:** via ferrata was suggested as "fun-scary", but one is on the declined list (`me/declined.md`, Torrent Falls; whether that means all of them is unasked). The Fishermen's Trail was suggested for summer.

### 2026-10-14 · The Atlantic North, deep dive
**Draft route** (⚠️ drive times not checked). First cut: Costa da Morte.
- **Days 1–2, Bilbao.** The post-industrial riverfront and the Bilbao La Vieja murals. The self-guided loop is ~5 km and 4–5 h with stops (📋 tourismattractions.net, 2026-05). The Zorrozaurre peninsula is the rawer, less curated version (same source). Artxanda: a 2.4 km walk up from the Salve bridge, or the funicular (📋 funicularartxanda.bilbao.eus).
- **Days 3–4, San Sebastián.** Pintxos crawl in the Parte Vieja. July hotel prices: €120–250/night in the Parte Vieja outside the festival, Gros 20–30% cheaper, hostel dorms €25–50 (📋 pixidia.com, 2026-05).
- **The Flysch Route, Zumaia → Deba** (GR-121 stage E05): **13.7 km, +626 m, ~4 h 45 min, no technical sections**, train back on Euskotren (📋 guiarutas.es, 2026-06; 📋 deba.eus says 14 km / 4 h). The cliffs are near-vertical rock layers recording 60+ million years. Algorri cove has the clay layer from the asteroid that killed the dinosaurs (the official reference section for that boundary is in El Kef, Tunisia, not here). Zumaia's Itzurun beach holds the official reference sections for two other ages of Earth history, ratified in 2008 (📋 guiarutas.es; 📋 geoparkea.eus). **The Sakoneta tidal rock platform is safe only at very low tide**, and you can be trapped by the incoming tide. In summer the Geopark runs guided boat trips between Zumaia and Deba (📋 zumaia.eus).
- **Gaztelugatxe:** free timed tickets are required on dates set by a yearly calendar; 2027's isn't out (📋 bizkaia.eus, tiketa.eus).
- **Days 5–7, Picos de Europa.** In 2026, **both car access to Poncebos (the Cares trailhead) and the Covadonga Lakes road were regulated Jun 1 – Oct 18**. Park in Arenas, take the bus to Poncebos (€1.55 each way, ~15 min, roughly hourly 8:15–13:00 and 17:00–19:00). The lakes road is closed to private cars, bus only. Ovar car park (146 spaces) is 1.2 km from Poncebos but not guaranteed (📋 turismoasturias.es; asturias.com, 2026-03; the federation site fempa.net, citing Spain's official gazette, Jan 20 2026). 2027 dates: TBD. *(was, in chat: "late July to early September", which was wrong for 2026)*
- **Ruta del Cares:** a path cut into the gorge wall for a hydro plant's workers (📋 roads-and-rivers.com). Distance and gain not confirmed: AllTrails name search returned only California trails. ⚠️ Recalled as ~12 km one way, Poncebos to Caín.
- **Descenso del Sella:** self-paddled canoe from Arriondas, 8 / 13.5 / 17 km, **€35/adult with gear and the shuttle back**, departures 11:00–14:00, must be able to swim. Book a few days ahead in July (📋 canoaselmoyau.com; aventuraenelsella.es, 2026-07). The International Sella Descent race day is in August, outside the window.
- **Weather:** Picos July normals are 23°C high, 16°C low, ~14 rainy days, 58 mm. June is wetter, ~17 days and 120 mm (📋 seasonmap.app). June 2026 was Asturias's hottest June on record, and two stations passed 40°C during a Saharan air intrusion Jun 21–24 (📋 AEMET, the Spanish weather agency, monthly summary). Rare, not zero.
- **Day 8, East Asturias coast:**
  - **Gulpiyuri:** a 40 m beach in a karst sinkhole ~100 m inland, fed by the sea through tunnels. A natural monument; park 200–300 m away and walk (📋 llanes.eu, 2025-07; barcelo.com).
  - **Bufones de Pría:** blowholes that only perform with high tide or big swell (📋 llanes.eu).
  - **Torimbia:** a crescent beach reached only on foot, ~10–15 min from the viewpoint car park; traditionally naturist, now mixed; good for sunset (📋 asturias.com; llanes.eu).
- **Days 9–10, Galicia:**
  - **Praia das Catedrais:** a free permit is required during Holy Week and **Jul 1 – Sep 30**. It opens 30 days ahead and sells out: 4 July 2026 days were full before the season opened. Go at low tide (📋 xunta.gal; turismoribadeo.gal; El Progreso, 2026-06-30; El Español, 2026-04). **Arriving Jun 28–30 means no permit is needed.**
  - **Costa da Morte:** Cabo Vilán (Spain's first electric lighthouse, 1896); the English Cemetery for HMS Serpent's 172 dead (1890); the Xallas waterfall at Ézaro, billed as Europe's only waterfall falling straight into the sea (📋 visitacostadamorte.com guide; vilanovaa.com, 2025-11).
  - **Ribeira Sacra** (the Sil canyon, ~500 m deep, with monasteries) is an inland alternative and probably doesn't fit (📋 turismo.gal).
- **Day 11, Santiago → Porto.** Bus ~2 h 10 min – 3 h 30 min, from ~€22 (FlixBus) or ~£8–30 (Alsa via Omio). Train is slower, 4 h 46 min+ (📋 FlixBus, Omio, Virail).
- **Days 12–13, Porto.** Mid-range €120–175/day; Cedofeita and Bonfim are cheaper and still walkable (📋 tripalta.com, 2026-07). City tourist tax €3/night (same source).

**Logistics**
- **Car:** pick up in Bilbao, **drop in Santiago, not Porto.** Spain → Portugal one-way fees are quoted at €100–300 on one spainautorental.com page and €300–600 on another: a contradiction, both 2026. Domestic one-way runs €50–250 (📋 spainautorental.com).
- **Under 25:**
  - Avis Spain: 21+, licence held 1 year+, €20.96–25/day **per under-25 driver**, charged for at most 10 days (📋 Avis Spain terms).
  - Hertz Spain: €31.76/day, capped at €444.68 per rental, and **vans require 25+** (📋 Hertz terms).
  - Manual is the default; under-25 plus automatic narrows options in peak season (📋 rentescar.com).
- **Flights:** no nonstop. Cleveland–Europe averages ~$1,254 round trip in June and ~$1,098 in July (📋 Kayak). Cleveland–Lisbon from ~$1,074 for July 20 (📋 brusselsairlines.com, 2026). Aer Lingus flies Cleveland–Dublin nonstop (📋 Kayak). **2027 fares TBD.**
- **Entry:** ETIAS (the EU's new travel authorization) launches Q4 2026: €20, valid 3 years; the passport must be valid 3 months past leaving Schengen (✅ travel-europe.europa.eu; 📋 eeas.europa.eu).

**Festivals inside the window**
- **San Fermín (Pamplona), Jul 6–14.** Encierros (the bull runs) start at 8:00 every morning Jul 7–14 (2026 dates; 📋 idealista.com). Pamplona rooms run €300–1,500/night. San Sebastián is the only base where a 5:00 bus gets you to the 8:00 run (📋 pixidia.com, 2026-05). Watching from the barriers is free. ⚠️ Running is past "fun-scary".
- **Bilbao BBK Live, Jul 8–10, 2027** (📋 bilbaobbklive.com header). Tickets were €78–190 in 2026 (📋 euskadi.eus).
- **So the direction matters.** Bilbao → Porto has you out of the Basque Country before both. Porto → Bilbao ends there during both: more money, more chaos.
