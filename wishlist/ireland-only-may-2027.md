His mom's side is **O'Dee**, so Clare covers that side of the family the way Skye covered McLeod.

## The places that aren't on every bus tour

- **Loop Head** (May 14): a headland and lighthouse 90 m above the Atlantic, with the Bridges of Ross sea arch and the Kilkee cliff walk on the same peninsula; a 1.1-mile cliff loop starts at the lighthouse car park; parking free at Kilkee and the lighthouse 📋 *(wildatlanticwayfarers.ie; komoot; cyclingholidays.ie)*. ⚠️ It adds roughly 2 h to a first day on the left: decide on the day.
- **Burren National Park** (May 16): free, open all year. Red, blue and green trails climb Mullaghmore, 2.5–3.5 h each. **No parking at the trailhead**; a free shuttle leaves the Corofin information point every 30 min from May ✅ *(nationalparks.ie/burren)*.
- **Dysert O'Dea Castle** (May 16), near Corofin: the O'Dea clan's 15th-century tower house, now the Clare Archaeology Centre 📋 *(odeaclan.org, discoverireland.ie)*. Its site said "Closed until May 2027" 📋 *(dysertcastle.ie)*: the opening date isn't shown. ~€5 📋 *(TripAdvisor review)*. ⚠️ O'Dee as a spelling of O'Dea is Claude's recollection, and Colin's own line isn't traced to Clare.
- **Diamond Hill** (May 18): 7 km loop, 4.6 mi and 1,236 ft, 2.5–3 h 📋 *(AllTrails; walkingholidayireland.com)*.
- **Omey Island** (May 19): a tidal island near Claddaghduff, reached across the sand at low tide only; ~6 km around, 1–2 h 📋 *(theculturetrip.com; community.ireland.com)*. ⚠️ Tide times decide the day.
- **Inishbofin** (May 19, instead): ferry from Cleggan Pier daily at 11:30 and again later in the day 📋 *(inishbofin.com)*. Fare not found.
- **Clonmacnoise** (May 20): the monastic site on the Shannon, €8 an adult; "avoid arriving between 10:30 and 15:00" ✅ *(heritageireland.ie)*.
- **Cliffs of Moher coastal walk:** part of the path between Doolin and the cliffs was closed for repairs from 2025 📋 *(hillwalktours.com, 2026-04)*. Status for May 2027 unknown.
