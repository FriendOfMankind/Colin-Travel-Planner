# Baja Gray Whales

*Wild whales that come to the boat — a friends trip, not a solo one*

## Why

📋 Each winter more than 1,000 gray whales pass through San Ignacio Lagoon, and only 16 small boats are allowed on it at a time. Whales often approach the boats close enough to touch *(sources: bajaex.com, racheloffduty.com, search snippets, 2026-10-05)*. Nothing like it is anywhere else on the list, and it only happens in late winter.

It's a guided camp and boat time, not hiking. `me/adventures.md` says guided outings are a reason to bring people, and wildlife is "a general inclination, not a trip driver". That makes this a **friends-trip candidate**, like Maui in 2028, rather than a solo spring break. Passport: owned *(stated 2026-10-03)*.

It passes the horizon test the easy way: a Feb–March week fits PTO in any year, so there's no rush.

### 2026-10-05 · Found while checking the Mar 5–15 window
- 📋 Season runs mid-January to mid-April; camps open mid-January *(source: Facebook group TalkBaja; tripadvisor, search snippets, 2026-10-05)*
- 📋 "It isn't until mid February to late March that the whale watching gets really good" *(source: oceanicsociety.org, search snippet, 2026-10-05)*. Some reviewers put the peak whale count at mid-February *(tripadvisor, snippet)*
- 📋 One operator lists ~90-minute boat sessions; a package example was 4 days / 3 nights with ~8 sessions *(sources: bajaex.com; TalkBaja, search snippets, 2026-10-05)*. Prices not recorded
- ⚠️ "Many whales never come near the boats all season" *(Baja Expeditions, Facebook, snippet)*. Touching is common, not guaranteed
- Compared against Sky Islands for spring break 2027: Sky Islands held for a solo hiking trip. This one is better with people *(daydream 2026-10-05)*
