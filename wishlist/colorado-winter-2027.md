## Research notes

### 2026-11-08 · From a Frisco road loop to skiing and frozen lakes
- First pass was a west/south road loop (Glenwood → Colorado NM → Black Canyon → Ouray → St. Elmo). Colin cut it: hot springs okay at best, no interest in red rock in snow, Black Canyon already done, ice climbing too pricey, a ghost town not worth a trip *(Colin, 2026-11-08)*. Facts found, for the record:
  - 📋 Ouray Ice Park "typically" opens mid to late December and closes mid-March *(source: ourayicepark.com FAQ, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*. Climbing there is free. Guided intro days: Skyward $275/climber; Alpine Institute private $555 (1:1), $350 each (2:1); San Juan Mountain Guides 2-day course $599 *(source: traveldurango.com/blog/ouray-ice-park-guide, read 2026-11-08)*
  - 📋 Colorado NM's Rim Rock Drive is open year round; the upper section closes during active storms and is cleared within 24 hours *(source: nps.gov/colm road information, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*
  - 📋 Black Canyon in winter: South Rim Drive past the visitor center is closed to cars and may be groomed as a 6-mile one-way ski and snowshoe trail; no rentals in the park *(source: nps.gov/blca winter activities, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*
  - 📋 St. Elmo: "the road is plowed year round" *(source: mtprinceton.com, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*. Ashcroft: Castle Creek Road plowed to the Ashcroft Ski Touring Center, 11 mi up *(source: aspennature.org, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*. Both are preserved, look-don't-enter towns, the drive-by kind under `me/principles.md` 10. The best explorable ruin near Frisco is still Mayflower Gulch, already on `wishlist/frisco-2026.md`
- 📋 **Devil's Thumb Ranch: "nearly 120km of trails"** at the base of the Continental Divide *(source: devilsthumbranch.com, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*. Listed as Colorado's biggest network, ahead of Aspen Nordic 90 km and Snow Mountain Ranch 82.5 km *(source: skiwise-app.com, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*
- 📋 **Snow Mountain Ranch: 82.5 km of groomed track and trail**, 40 km pet-friendly *(source: winterparkresort.com, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*. One Reddit local slightly prefers it over Devil's Thumb *(source: r/skiing, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*
- ⚠️ Trail pass and rental prices at both: not checked
- 📋 **Rocky Mountain NP needs no timed-entry reservation from November to late May** *(source: kirksflyshop.com, search snippet, 2026-11-08; nps.gov blocked here, verify)*
- 📋 **Emerald Lake** sits at ~10,110 ft past Nymph and Dream lakes. Round-trip distance disagrees between sources: 3.4 mi *(roadtripsforfamilies.com, snippet)* vs 7.8 mi *(sojournermountainguides.com, snippet)*. Locals: microspikes are usually enough for Emerald and Mills lakes; deeper snow needs snowshoes *(source: Facebook group, search snippet, 2026-11-08)*. Check AllTrails before trusting either number
- ⚠️ **Sky Pond in winter**, ~10 mi, past Mills Lake and The Loch: Timberline Falls becomes a frozen step. Avalanche exposure not researched. Check CAIC (avalanche.state.co.us) the morning of, and don't plan it as a sure thing
- 📋 Optional ice stop, the wrong direction for this route: **Rifle Mountain Park ice caves**, frozen waterfalls you walk inside on a solid ice floor, microspikes essential; $10/vehicle *(sources: everydaylaura.com and rexby.com, search snippets, 2026-11-08)*
- ⚠️ Winter driving: I-70 over Vail Pass and Berthoud Pass (if used) close in storms; AWD rental, and a buffer day before the flight
