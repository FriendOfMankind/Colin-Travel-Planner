# Grand Staircase–Escalante

*Warm desert water, which barely exists out west*

## Why

⚠️ Lower Calf Creek, the Zebra and Tunnel slots, Devil's Garden and Willis Creek all still work as day hikes. Warm swimmable desert water is genuinely rare out west, where most water is 37–50°F. ⚠️ The centrepiece — Coyote Gulch via Hurricane Wash — is out under the no-backpacking rule, so this is a lesser version of the trip that was researched.

### 2026-10-03 · Free camping and showers
- Context: Colin daydreaming a dispersed-first drive-out western loop, showering at places that sell one. Not a standing preference *(stated 2026-10-03; `me/camping.md` still says developed + showers)*
- 📋 Hole-in-the-Rock Road: dispersed BLM camping, no permit, starting about 5 mi from Escalante *(source: thedyrt.com, reddit r/CampingandHiking, youtube, search snippets, 2026-10-03)*. "Most passenger vehicles can handle the first few miles" in dry weather; mud after rain *(source: thedyrt.com, search snippet, 2026-10-03)*
- 📋 Showers: Escalante Outfitters (campers get hot showers; whether they sell to non-guests is not confirmed) *(source: escalanteoutfitters.com/lodging, search snippet, 2026-10-03)*. Alternative reported: Escalante Petrified Forest State Park day-use pass, about $8, then use the showers *(source: tripadvisor forum, search snippet, 2026-10-03)*. Both unverified
- ⚠️ A rain day makes the dispersed road a trap for a low sedan, so the fallback should be a paved campground
