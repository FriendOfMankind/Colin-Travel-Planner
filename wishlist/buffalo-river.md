# Buffalo National River

*The penciled 2027 shoulder trip*

## Why

✅ The only dam-free river of its kind and the best warm swimming on the whole list. April is peak waterfall and wildflower season and the upper river is only navigable in spring. Hemmed-In Hollow is the tallest waterfall between the Rockies and the Appalachians; Whitaker Point, Big Bluff and Lost Valley all day-hike from a drive-up base. ⚠️ **Kyles Landing fails the clearance test** — 3 miles of very steep gravel, 4WD recommended. Steel Creek is the base: 26 tent-only sites under Roark Bluff, water and flush restrooms roughly Mar 15–Nov 15.
